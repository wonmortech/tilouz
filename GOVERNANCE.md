# Governance

Tilo is a non-profit, open-source project hosted in the public `wonmortech` GitHub organization
([Wonmor](https://wonmor.io)).

## Principle: Deaf-led

"Nothing about us without us." Tilo is built with and for Deaf, hard-of-hearing and non-speaking people.
The target structure is written in [docs/06_Platforma_rejasi/06d_Boshqaruv_jamoa_va_hamkorlar.md](docs/06_Platforma_rejasi/06d_Boshqaruv_jamoa_va_hamkorlar.md):

- a steering board with a **Deaf majority**;
- a Deaf product lead who decides product priorities;
- paid Deaf contributors for sign content and testing (no unpaid sign-language labour);
- a regional advisory council.

**Status today:** the project is being set up and these bodies do not exist yet. Until they do, the maintainers
below run the repository, and every product decision that affects Deaf users is opened as a public issue for
community feedback.

## Roles

| Role | Who | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| Project lead, maintainer | [@xnosir](https://github.com/xnosir) | Review and merge, triage, releases, security reports |
| Maintainer (organization owner) | [@wonmors](https://github.com/wonmors) | Same |
| Contributor | Anyone who opens an issue or a pull request | Propose changes; becomes a maintainer after sustained, reviewed contributions |

## Decisions

Day-to-day decisions happen in issues and pull requests. Larger decisions (scope, licences, data and consent
policy, architecture) are proposed in an issue, stay open at least 7 days, and are recorded in `docs/`.
Decisions about sign-language content and data need review by Deaf community members.
