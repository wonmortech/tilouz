# Uzbekistan: Deaf / Hard-of-Hearing / Non-Speaking Community Landscape (as of Nov 2026)

## 1. Statistics: how many deaf / hard-of-hearing people, children, cochlear implants

### Takeaway
No current official national count of people with hearing disability is published. The commonly cited number is about **21,000–23,000 people registered with the Uzbekistan Society of the Deaf**, plus about 8,000 children on its registry. All registered disability groups together total about 1.05 million (2.7% of the population, 2025). The real number of people with hearing loss is almost certainly much higher, because these figures count registry members only.

### Cited Findings
- **2025:** the Uzbekistan Society of the Deaf has "over 22,000 members" and nearly **8,000 children with hearing disabilities** on its registry. There are no recent official statistics on hearing impairment, and the National Agency for Social Protection reports disability only by the three disability groups (I/II/III), not by type — [Gazeta.uz, 30.09.2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **July 2025 (Russian edition of the same article):** "more than 22 thousand people with hearing disability, about 8,000 in Tashkent region alone" — [Gazeta.uz RU, 28.09.2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/09/28/sign-language/) (search snippet)
- **2019:** **21,212** people with hearing impairments were registered with the Society of the Deaf. Goskomstat publishes no separate figure, and many people are believed to be unregistered — [Dilmurad Yusupov, 23.09.2020](https://dilmurad.me/expert/uzbek-sign-language-usl-to-be-or-not-to-be/); [Wikipedia: Deafness in Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deafness_in_Uzbekistan)
- **Undated Uzbek government answer to OHCHR (likely about 2019–2020):** Society of the Deaf branch data list **18,303 deaf and 3,875 hard-of-hearing people**, 22,178 in total — [OHCHR document, Permanent Mission of Uzbekistan](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/Documents/Issues/Disability/ProvisionSupport/States/Permanent_Mission_of_Uzbekistan.doc) (search snippet)
- **2020:** the Society's central board said nearly **10,000** people aged 16+, about 47% of all registered deaf people, were Society members — [Gazeta.uz, 30.06.2020](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2020/06/30/deaf-and-dumb/) (search snippet). A critical opinion piece by a Society member put active members at **fewer than 1,500** — [Hook.report, Nov 2020](https://old.hook.report/2020/11/bizsiz-biz-haqimizda-hech-narsa/)
- **2021:** an unofficial estimate puts hearing-impaired citizens at over 23,000. The State Statistics Committee does not track the number precisely — [Daryo, 07.10.2021](https://daryo.uz/2021/10/07/jamiyat-bizni-oziga-ogay-sanaydi-eshitish-va-gapirish-imkoniyati-cheklanganlar-ham-sifatli-talim-yaxshi-sharoit-izlab-toshkentga-kelishga-majbur-shifoko/)
- **All disabilities, 2025:** **1,031,609** persons with disabilities at the end of Q1 2025 (2.7% of the population), including **173,285 children under 18**. The total was 1,050,218 on 1 July 2025 and **1,055,770** on 1 August 2025. Samarqand region has the most (110,189), Sirdaryo the fewest (38,123) — [Gazeta.uz, 15.04.2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/04/15/disability/); [Daryo, 11.07.2025](https://daryo.uz/2025/07/11/ozbekistonda-aholining-necha-foizini-nogironlar-tashkil-qilishi-malum-boldi/); [Qalampir](https://qalampir.uz/uz/news/uzbekistondagi-nogironligi-bor-shakhslar-soni-ochik-landi-117480)
- **2019:** about **5,000** deaf and hard-of-hearing students attended specialised deaf schools — [Wikipedia: Deafness in Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deafness_in_Uzbekistan)
- **Cochlear implants:** the Republican Specialised Scientific-Practical Medical Centre of Paediatrics estimates the need at **300–350 newborns per year**. Older reporting says 300–400 children a year are born with congenital deafness or hearing loss. A 2020 plan was for **320 children** to receive implants in 2020. In 2015, implants were bought by the state and given free to children under 5, and the operation was free for under-18s (dated, needs re-checking) — [search summary of Gazeta.uz 2015](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2015/11/16/operations/), [anhor.uz](https://anhor.uz/ru/news/fond-zamin-nachal-rabotu-po-programme-besplatnoy-kohlearnoy-implantacii-dlya-detey-s-narusheniyami-s)
- **Zamin Foundation (First Lady's foundation):** runs a "Cochlear implantation for school-age children" programme for boarding-school pupils. 36 operations were planned after diagnostics (33 cochlear, 3 bone-conduction), with more than 30 more planned for the next year. Separate reports cover batches of 10 and 12 operations (exact dates not confirmed) — [UzDaily](https://www.uzdaily.uz/ru/fond-zamin-10-operatsii-v-ramkakh-programmy-kokhlearnaia-implantatsiia-dlia-detei-shkolnogo-vozrasta/); [UzDaily](https://www.uzdaily.uz/ru/provedeny-12-operatsii-v-ramkakh-programmy-fonda-zamin/); [UzNews](https://www.uznews.uz/ru/news/7960)
- **2021:** at one specialised boarding school, **239 pupils needed hearing aids** (Child Rights Ombudsman) — [ombudsman.uz, 16.04.2021](https://ombudsman.uz/oz/news/2021/04/16/bola-huquqlari-boyicha-vakil-ixtisoslashtirilgan-maktab-internatida-239-nafar-tarbiyalanuvchi-eshitish-apparatlariga-muhtoj)

### Inferences
- "22,000" is best read as **Society registry members (deaf + hard-of-hearing, registered)**, not prevalence. WHO global ratios (about 5% of people with disabling hearing loss) applied to roughly 37–38 million people would imply far more people with hearing loss, mostly older and hard-of-hearing, who are not sign language users. The core sign-language-using audience is likely in the low tens of thousands, and the wider audience that needs captions is in the hundreds of thousands or more. This is an inference, not a sourced figure.
- With about 300+ congenitally deaf births a year and an ongoing cochlear implant programme, a growing cohort of implanted children will need captioning and speech support rather than sign language.

### Gaps
- No WHO country-specific estimate for Uzbekistan was found in this pass.
- No 2024–2026 official count of the hearing disability category from the National Agency for Social Protection or the Statistics Agency.
- No cumulative 2024–2026 national cochlear implant count was found.

## 2. Organizations, schools, communities

### Takeaway
The central actor is the **Uzbekistan Society of the Deaf** (O'zbekiston Karlar jamiyati, O'zKJ; TIN 200849599), a Soviet-era body founded about 1929, with a Culture Centre and the only Mimika and Sign Language Theatre in Tashkent. The most active advocacy NGO is **Sharoit Plyus** (Dilmurad Yusupov, Mamur Ahliddinov). There are about 18–19 specialised boarding schools for deaf and hard-of-hearing children.

### Cited Findings
- **Society of the Deaf, legal entity:** "O'ZBEKISTON KARLAR JAMIYATI…", TIN 200849599 — [orginfo.uz](https://orginfo.uz/en/organization/97f86baf947b/)
- **Society of the Deaf, 95th anniversary in 2024:** the Society celebrated its 95th anniversary in 2024, which implies it was founded in about 1929. Its theatre troupe toured all regions for the anniversary — [Gazeta.uz, 29.09.2026](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2026/09/29/theater-of-mimicry-and-gesture/)
- **Culture centre and theatre:**
  - The Republican Culture Palace opened in 1973. The Olmazor district area around it became the "deaf town" (gorodok glukhikh).
  - In 2001 it became the Culture Centre for Deaf and Deaf-Blind People.
  - The theatre has a permanent troupe of 15 and a 400-seat hall on Qorasaroy street. Tickets cost about 30,000 soum. It is chronically underfunded: no stable salaries, and it declined a trip to Kazakhstan for lack of funds.
  - Interpreter Kamila Yakubjanova voices the performances. Deputy director: Oleg Oshepkov.
  — [Gazeta.uz, 29.09.2026](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2026/09/29/theater-of-mimicry-and-gesture/)
- **Soviet and post-Soviet history:** Society enterprises had supply monopolies under the USSR. After 1991 they lost funding and went bankrupt, and many deaf people lost their jobs and homes. Today about 10 training facilities serve only 240–250 deaf people nationwide. The Culture Centre received JICA support (a 2014 award, and the 2018 Asian Deaf-blind Conference in Japan) — [Wikipedia: Deafness in Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deafness_in_Uzbekistan)
- **Governance problems (2017–2020):**
  - In 2017 the Society suspended its membership in the **World Federation of the Deaf** because of debts and unpaid fees.
  - Chairperson **Javohir Rikhsiev** (spelled Zhavokhir Rykhsiev in the source) rejected a 2017 proposal for a subtitled sign-language channel.
  - The Society says it has no funds for sign language development or interpreters.
  — [Dilmurad Yusupov, 2020](https://dilmurad.me/expert/uzbek-sign-language-usl-to-be-or-not-to-be/)
  - A critique titled "Nothing about us without us: why are hearing people running the deaf?" was published in 2020 — [Hook.report](https://old.hook.report/2020/11/bizsiz-biz-haqimizda-hech-narsa/)
- **Sharoit Plyus NGO (Tashkent):**
  - Advocacy group for people with disabilities. Contacts: info@sharoitplus.uz, +998 55 500-50-60, Telegram t.me/sharoitplus. Authors on its site include Dilmurad Yusupov and Saida Turabekova.
  - It ran a tender for the **Uzbek Sign Language (O'IIT) online dictionary website and mobile app**, with an 11-week development window, and published tender results.
  — [Sharoit Plus tender results](https://sharoitplus.uz/ozbek-imo-ishora-tili-oiit-onlayn-lugati-uchun-veb-sayt-va-mobil-ilovani-ishlab-chiqish-boyicha-tender-natijalari/); [Telegram](https://t.me/s/sharoitplus); [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **Other organizations:**
  - The "Yuksalish" nationwide movement surveyed 102 hearing-impaired people (2024) — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
  - "Inson" social service centres (National Agency for Social Protection) are proposed as delivery points for interpreter services — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **Schools:**
  - About **18** specialised institutions for deaf and hard-of-hearing children, out of 86 special institutions with 28,159 pupils — [uzedu.uz](https://www.uzedu.uz/uz/news/130) (date unclear)
  - An academic paper says there are **19** boarding schools — [search summary](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391882672_ESHITISHIDA_NUQSONI_BO'LGAN_BOLALAR_TA'LIMINING_RIVOJLANISH_TARIXI)
  - In 2000/01 there were 13 deaf and 5 hard-of-hearing boarding schools. Schools are split into type I (deaf) and type II (hard-of-hearing and late-deafened), under Cabinet of Ministers resolution No. 256 of 13.09.2011 — [lex.uz 256-son](https://lex.uz/docs/-1866997)
  - Boarding school No. 102 in Tashkent is named in the 2026 theatre article — [Gazeta.uz 2026](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2026/09/29/theater-of-mimicry-and-gesture/)
- **Teaching in deaf schools:** many teachers do not know sign language, and instruction is oralist (lip reading and speech). Pupils graduate one year later than in mainstream schools — [Yusupov 2020](https://dilmurad.me/expert/uzbek-sign-language-usl-to-be-or-not-to-be/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deafness_in_Uzbekistan)
- **Deaf bloggers:** Mamur Ahliddinov is a deaf activist, blogger and Tashkent State Law University master's student, and co-author of the 2025 Gazeta column — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)

### Inferences
- The Society holds the registry and the legitimacy but has weak capacity and finances. Sharoit Plyus and individual deaf activists are the most likely early partners and co-designers ("nothing about us without us").
- About 18–19 boarding schools form a concentrated, reachable distribution channel for a children and families product.

### Gaps
- No confirmed list of regional Society branches (count and addresses) or current leadership in 2026.
- No systematic list of deaf-led YouTube or Telegram channels and their subscriber counts was found. Search for them directly on Telegram (e.g. "karlar", "glukhie Uzbekistan").
- No deaf-led startups were identified.

## 3. Sign language: status, dictionaries, interpreters, training

### Takeaway
Uzbek deaf people use a **variety of Russian Sign Language**, usually called "Uzbek Sign Language" (o'zbek imo-ishora tili, O'IIT, UzSL). Its legal status is only a "means of interpersonal communication". There is **no official interpreter profession or standard**, about **10 interpreters for about 5,000 deaf people in Tashkent**, and the state dictionary deadline (1 June 2024) was missed. University interpreter degrees started in 2023.

### Cited Findings
- **Relation to RSL:** RSL is the primary base. The Uzbek variant has drifted since 1991 into a dialect of RSL, and whether it is a separate "UZL" is unresolved. Kyrgyz, Tajik and Uzbek signing differ little from Russian RSL — [Yusupov 2020](https://dilmurad.me/expert/uzbek-sign-language-usl-to-be-or-not-to-be/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deafness_in_Uzbekistan)
- **Legal status:** Article 28 of Law O'RQ-641 recognises sign language only as a "means of communication". There is no stronger status in law (as of Sept 2025) — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/); [lex.uz O'RQ-641](https://lex.uz/docs/-5049511?ONDATE=09.09.2024)
- **Draft Cabinet resolution (never confirmed adopted):** a draft resolution "On further development of sign language" would have:
  - recognised O'IIT across the country as the language of communication for people with severe hearing or speech impairments;
  - obliged all organisations to create conditions for its use;
  - had the Ministry of Public Education develop a **Latin-script Uzbek dactyl alphabet**;
  - had deadlines referencing 2021/22 (likely a 2020–21 draft).
  — [Kun.uz 27059463](https://kun.uz/27059463) (page date ambiguous)
- **PQ-407 of 20.10.2022, "On additional measures to develop Uzbek sign language and Braille"** (in force 21.10.2022). From 1 Jan 2023:
  - free sign language and Braille courses at schools and **"Ishga marhamat" monocenters** for people with disabilities and their parents, with online registration;
  - **at least 5% of state TV weekly airtime** with sign interpretation, closed subtitles or a running text crawl;
  - mandatory sign interpreters at university entrance exams for deaf applicants;
  - waiver of the foreign-language certificate for master's admission;
  - a 25% higher grant quota in surdopedagogy and tiflopedagogy for 2023/24;
  - from 2024/25, deaf schools hire only teachers who know sign language;
  - an annual September **"Best sign language interpreter" contest** (prizes of 100/65/50 BHM).
  — [norma.uz](https://www.norma.uz/oz/qonunchilikda_yangi/uzbek_imo-ishora_tili_va_brayl_alifbosini_rivojlantirish_buyicha_chora-tadbirlar_belgilandi)
- **Dictionary deadline:** PQ-407 tasked a linguistic dictionary and training materials by **1 June 2024**, and the deadline was missed. NGO Sharoit Plyus then took on the online dictionary (web and app) — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/). The norma.uz summary does not list the dictionary item, so the dictionary may be in an annex. This is a minor source discrepancy.
- **Interpreter numbers:** about 10 interpreters for about 5,000 deaf people in Tashkent city and region, roughly 2 per 1,000 (Russia about 3 per 1,000, Finland about 300 per 1,000). Interpreters are mostly CODAs (children of deaf adults), poorly paid and overworked — [Yusupov 2020](https://dilmurad.me/expert/uzbek-sign-language-usl-to-be-or-not-to-be/)
  - 2025: "fewer than 10 qualified interpreters in the capital", or one per 800 deaf people — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
  - The Society has very few qualified interpreters, only one with higher education — [yuz.uz](https://yuz.uz/ru/news/inklyuzivnoe-obrazovanie-kak-obespechivaetsya-ravenstvo-prav-i-vozmojnostey-dlya-lits-s-invalidnostyu) (undated snippet)
- **Profession and standard:** there is no official profession "Uzbek sign language interpreter", no national service standard and no guaranteed free hours. Russia guarantees 40 hours a year and Kazakhstan 30 — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **University training:**
  - From **2023**, a bachelor's degree "Maxsus pedagogika: surdotarjimonlik faoliyati" runs at the National Pedagogical University (Nizomiy TDPU) and other universities, with first graduates expected around 2026 — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
  - Tashkent State Medical University (TMA) advertised a 4-year **"Surdotarjimonlik"** programme in its 2026 admissions — [admissions.tma.uz, June 2026](https://admissions.tma.uz/2026/06/inson-qalbiga-yol-imo-ishora-tili-orqali-%F0%9F%91%90/)
  - TDPU teaching materials on dactylology and sign speech segmentation (Z.N. Mamarajabova) — [devedu.uz PDF](https://devedu.uz/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/3-Daktilologiya-va-imo-ishora-nutqi.pdf)
- **Other training:**
  - July 2025: the first International Sign course was held in Uzbekistan — [Gazeta.uz RU, 15.07.2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/07/15/sign-language/)
  - May 2026: training of sign-language tour guides ("surdogid") is being expanded for deaf tourists — [nuz.uz, 16.05.2026](https://nuz.uz/2026/05/16/v-uzbekistane-rasshiryayut-podgotovku-surdogidov-dlya-turistov-s-narusheniem-sluha/)
- **Courses:** the 2017 presidential order for sign language courses was never implemented. PQ-407 (2022) re-created them, and a 2022 decree (lex.uz/docs/-6248467) set up 3-month courses at "Ishga marhamat" monocenters — [Yusupov 2020](https://dilmurad.me/expert/uzbek-sign-language-usl-to-be-or-not-to-be/); [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)

### Inferences
- With roughly 1 interpreter per 500–800 deaf people and no state-funded hours, **remote video interpretation (VRI) on demand and a booking marketplace** directly address the largest documented gap. A deaf respondent explicitly asked for a "single interpreter database and online service".
- Any sign-language AI must be trained on **RSL-based Uzbek signing** with local variants. Russian RSL resources (datasets, dictionaries) are partly transferable, which is a key technical shortcut.
- University programmes graduating from about 2026 create a new supply of interpreters who need jobs, which suits a platform marketplace model.

### Gaps
- Whether the Sharoit Plyus dictionary app has actually launched (store listing, number of signs) was not confirmed.
- There is no official national count of certified interpreters, because no certification exists.
- No adoption record for the sign-language-status Cabinet resolution was found on lex.uz.

## 4. Laws, government services, emergency access, responsible agencies

### Takeaway
The legal base is **Law O'RQ-641 of 15.10.2020**, the UN CRPD (ratified 2021) and PQ-407 (2022). Implementation is weak. A deaf emergency dispatch service has existed since 2019 (video and SMS via JusTalk), Surdo-online tablets are in some service centres, and the main agency is the **National Agency for Social Protection under the President** (created 2023, with "Inson" centres in every district).

### Cited Findings
- **Law O'RQ-641 of 15.10.2020**, "On the rights of persons with disabilities". It provides that state bodies hire sign interpreters by contract when needed to communicate with a deaf person — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/acts/-5049511); [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **UN CRPD:** signed in 2009 and not ratified as of 2020 — [Yusupov 2020](https://dilmurad.me/expert/uzbek-sign-language-usl-to-be-or-not-to-be/). Ratified in **2021** — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **TV:** PQ-407 sets at least 5% of weekly state-TV airtime as accessible — [norma.uz](https://www.norma.uz/oz/qonunchilikda_yangi/uzbek_imo-ishora_tili_va_brayl_alifbosini_rivojlantirish_buyicha_chora-tadbirlar_belgilandi)
  - In practice (2025), there are about 25 channels. "O'zbekiston 24" has signed news only at 14:00 and 15:00, 15–20 minutes in total. Other channels sign only occasionally, and subtitles are almost absent on TV, YouTube and social media — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
  - Friday sermons have been signed since 25.01.2019 — [Yusupov 2020](https://dilmurad.me/expert/uzbek-sign-language-usl-to-be-or-not-to-be/)
- **Interpreters at citizen receptions:** state bodies must ensure a sign interpreter takes part when they receive citizens — [UzDaily](https://www.uzdaily.uz/ru/pri-provedenii-priema-grazhdan-v-obiazatelnom-poriadke-budet-obespechivatsia-uchastie-surdoperevodchika/) (date not confirmed)
- **Emergency dispatch:** a 24/7 dispatch service for people with hearing and speech impairments has run under the Ministry of Emergency Situations since **1 July 2019** (presidential decree), accepting video calls, SMS and specialised software. Users report the **JusTalk** video app often fails, and there is no domestic app — [Yusupov 2020](https://dilmurad.me/expert/uzbek-sign-language-usl-to-be-or-not-to-be/)
  - Uztelecom infrastructure is the base for the national unified 112 service (2024–25). It is unconfirmed whether deaf channels are integrated into 112 — [Wikipedia: Uztelecom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uztelecom) (search snippet)
  - During COVID-19, deaf people could not reliably reach the 1197 hotline — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **Surdo-Online:** a remote interpretation hotline with tablets in pharmacies, banks, railway ticket offices and other institutions that signed contracts. In November 2020 it launched in 11 Tashkent district offices. Some tablets do not work: in one bank, staff could not connect a call — [Yusupov 2020](https://dilmurad.me/expert/uzbek-sign-language-usl-to-be-or-not-to-be/); [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **Courts and notaries:**
  - Courts: interpretation must be requested in advance and is often paid by the deaf person — [World Bank CRPD technical notes](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052223112035849/pdf/P1783520faee9f09d0a46307d56768f9126.pdf) (snippet)
  - Notaries: the Ministry of Justice notarial instruction of 04.01.2019 (No. 3113) requires a sign interpreter if a deaf person is illiterate — [nrm.uz](https://nrm.uz/contentf?doc=578912_&products=1_vse_zakonodatelstvo_uzbekistana)
- **Responsible agency:** the **National Agency for Social Protection under the President** (Ijtimoiy himoya milliy agentligi, created 2023), with "Inson" social service centres in each district, social worker teams from 1 July 2023, and the "Yagona milliy ijtimoiy himoya" information system — [gov.uz](https://gov.uz/oz/advice/720/document/3119); [Kun.uz 02.06.2023](https://kun.uz/kr/news/2023/06/02/ijtimoiy-himoya-milliy-agentligi-rejalari-va-vazifalari-belgilandi)
  - Its predecessor, the **Agency for the Development of Medical and Social Services** (created Dec 2018 under the Ministry of Health), was named in PQ-407 for course registration and the interpreter contest — [Kun.uz 2018](https://kun.uz/news/2018/12/08/soglikni-saklas-vazirligi-uzurida-tibbij-iztimoij-hizmatlar-agentligi-taskil-etilmokda); [norma.uz](https://www.norma.uz/oz/qonunchilikda_yangi/uzbek_imo-ishora_tili_va_brayl_alifbosini_rivojlantirish_buyicha_chora-tadbirlar_belgilandi)
  - The "Faol hayotga qadam" programme is proposed as a channel for interpretation services — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)

### Inferences
- The regulatory hooks for a platform are PQ-407 (TV 5% accessibility, hiring sign-literate teachers), O'RQ-641 (state bodies must provide interpreters by contract) and the citizen-reception rule. **State bodies are obliged buyers without a working supply chain**, which makes B2G (business-to-government) VRI a credible revenue path.
- The emergency channel (JusTalk-based, unreliable) is a concrete, documented failure that a domestic app could replace, for example in partnership with the Ministry of Emergency Situations or 112.

### Gaps
- No evidence found on whether my.gov.uz or public service centres meet any accessibility standard (WCAG, sign-language video help) in 2024–2026.
- The current 2026 status of the deaf emergency dispatch line (number, app, integration with 112) is unknown.
- No 2026 Surdo-Online operator details (company, price, coverage) were found.

## 5. Existing local tech: research, apps, datasets, speech recognition

### Takeaway
Uzbek sign language recognition work is **academic and prototype-level**: YOLO dactyl alphabet, MediaPipe with Random Forest, BiLSTM, CNN-LSTM vs Transformer, and GitHub prototypes of about 50 signs. There is **no public large Uzbek sign language dataset**. Uzbek speech recognition is mature and commercially available (Aisha, Muxlisa/UZINFOCOM, UzbekVoice, Yandex, Google), so captioning is technically ready now.

### Cited Findings
- **IEEE, "An Algorithm of Recognition Uzbek Sign Language" (about Nov 2024):** YOLO-based recognition of the dactyl (fingerspelling) alphabet on a self-built image dataset — [IEEE 10956783](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10956783/); [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/390790430_An_Algorithm_of_Recognition_Uzbek_Sign_Language). A related ResearchGate listing by F. Akhmedov, M. Umarov, E. Urinov and B. Turgunov reports a BiLSTM with MediaPipe reaching "100%" test accuracy, which is suspicious and likely reflects a small test set.
- **IEEE, "Assistance to Uzbek people with speaking disabilities using ML and Sign Language Detection":** MediaPipe + scikit-learn Random Forest. The authors note dataset creation is hard — [IEEE 10544372](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10544372/)
- **Zenodo 2026:** CNN-LSTM (87%) vs Squeezeformer-style Transformer (91%) for isolated-sign recognition, on a proprietary landmark dataset, not tested signer-independent — [Zenodo 22840569](https://zenodo.org/records/22840569)
- **Signly.uz (Jasurbek Ergashev, Sept 2026):** Flutter + Firebase gamified UzSL and ASL learning app, with offline TFLite recognition of a subset of a "500+ sign" companion dataset. Piloted with 10 coursemates, no accuracy reported — [Zenodo 24003015](https://zenodo.org/records/24003015); [reandpub.uz](https://reandpub.uz/konferensiya/article/view/598)
- **Other papers:** a review of HMM, SVM, k-NN and deep learning methods for UzSL — [Zenodo 10093343](https://zenodo.org/records/10093343); an "Uzbek sign language classifier based on ML" — [eipublication](https://eipublication.com/index.php/eijmrms/article/view/1885)
- **GitHub prototypes:**
  - khdbeck/uzslr-sign-language-prototype: 50 isolated UzSL signs, CNN-Transformer in PyTorch, real time — [GitHub](https://github.com/khdbeck/uzslr-sign-language-prototype)
  - umid-weeb/uzbek-sign-language-ai: RTMPose + Random Forest, real time — [GitHub](https://github.com/umid-weeb/uzbek-sign-language-ai)
- **Regional dataset:** FluentSigners-50, a signer-independent Kazakh-Russian sign language benchmark (Kazakhstan, RSL-family, transferable) — [PMC9467305](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9467305/)
- **Kaggle:** no Uzbek sign language dataset was found. Uzbek speech and text datasets exist — [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/javokhirraimov/uzbek-language-dataset-sentence-and-audio)
- **Speech corpora:** USC open-source Uzbek Speech Corpus (ISSAI, about 105 hours) — [arXiv 2107.14419](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.14419); FeruzaSpeech, 60 hours — [arXiv 2410.00035](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.00035)
- **Speech recognition and synthesis providers:**
  - **Aisha AI** (Tashkent): Uzbek, Russian and English STT with speaker identification, a free tier and pay-as-you-go in so'm — [aisha.group](https://aisha.group/en/uzbek-speech-recognition)
  - **Muxlisa AI** (state company UZINFOCOM): Uzbek and Karakalpak STT and TTS, live and mixed Uzbek-Russian speech. Listed prices: STT 350 UZS, TTS 650 UZS per 1,000 characters — [muxlisa.uz](https://muxlisa.uz/en); [awesome-uzbek-ai](https://github.com/braindiggeruz/awesome-uzbek-ai)
  - **UzbekVoice**: STT, TTS (Shoira and Jasur voices) and Studio subtitles and dubbing (vendor confirmation not found)
  - Yandex SpeechKit uz-UZ, Google Cloud STT, Azure, ElevenLabs Scribe and Whisper are also available
  — [awesome-uzbek-ai](https://github.com/braindiggeruz/awesome-uzbek-ai)
- **Ecosystem:** the "Voice AI Challenge Uzbekistan" hackathon (Dec 2021) was run by the IT Ministry (MITC), IT Park and UNDP. It produced the uzbekvoice.ai corpus effort — [IT Park](https://www.it-park.uz/en/itpark/news/in-uzbekistan-mitc-it-park-and-undp-organize-hackathon-called-voice-ai-challenge-uzbekistan); [UNDP](https://www.undp.org/uzbekistan/stories/young-people-develop-ai-solutions-uzbek-language); [corpus.uzbekvoice.ai](https://corpus.uzbekvoice.ai/en-US/yangilik/1). A Second National AI Hackathon was held in Samarkand — [outsource.gov.uz](https://www.outsource.gov.uz/en/media/the-second-national-ai-hackathon-will-take-place-in-samarkand). Tilmoch AI is an Uzbek translation startup — [startupbase.uz](https://startupbase.uz/en/news/tilmoch-ai-an-uzbek-startup-changing-the-future-of)

### Inferences
- **Speech-to-text captioning (Uzbek, Russian and code-switched speech) is buildable today** on Aisha or Muxlisa APIs at low cost, for example Muxlisa at about 350 UZS per 1,000 characters.
- Sign-to-text is the hard, unsolved part. Existing work covers the fingerspelling alphabet or 50–500 isolated signs with no signer-independent evaluation. A platform could own the **first large, consented UzSL video dataset** (with the Society, Sharoit Plyus, boarding schools and the new interpreter-degree students) as a moat and a research partnership.
- Authors such as Akhmedov, Umarov and Turgunov are probably at TATU or other Tashkent institutions, but affiliations were not verified.

### Gaps
- University affiliations (TATU, Inha, Westminster, the Digital Technologies and AI Research Institute) of the sign language papers were not confirmed.
- No IT Park-resident startup focused on deaf users was identified.
- No public UzSL dataset with a licence was found on GitHub, Kaggle or Hugging Face in this pass.

## 6. Funding and partners

### Takeaway
The active donor channel is the **UN joint disability programme (UNPRPD: UNICEF, UNDP, UNFPA, OHCHR)** with the social protection agency. ## 7. Pain points reported by Uzbek deaf people

### Takeaway
The documented pains are concentrated:
- interpreters are unaffordable or unavailable, especially outside Tashkent and in emergencies;
- banks, hospitals, schools and universities have no interpreters on site;
- the Surdo-online tablets often fail, and so does the emergency video line;
- there is no captioning on TV or online;
- literacy gaps make text a poor substitute.

### Cited Findings
- **Yuksalish survey (102 respondents, 2024):** 56% often face difficulties using sign language, about 90% regularly or occasionally, and only 6% never — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **Interpreter access:** interpreters are expensive (compared to paying for an English interpreter), must be booked in advance, and are unavailable urgently. There is no single interpreter database or online service, and no interpreter-operators in **banks, schools, hospitals** — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **Bank:** staff could not connect the Surdo-online tablet call — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **Universities:** deaf students study without interpreters and rely on classmates — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **Literacy:** some deaf people have limited literacy, so written communication is inadequate — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **Courts, police, tax, medical assessment:** interpreters support deaf people in all of these and face burnout. Courts require advance requests and often payment — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deafness_in_Uzbekistan); [World Bank](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052223112035849/pdf/P1783520faee9f09d0a46307d56768f9126.pdf)
- **Emergency:** the JusTalk-based video channel fails — [Yusupov 2020](https://dilmurad.me/expert/uzbek-sign-language-usl-to-be-or-not-to-be/). The COVID hotline 1197 was unreachable — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- **Medical error:** a Tashkent designer lost his hearing through a doctor's error and says "society treats us as stepchildren". Deaf people move to Tashkent for education and conditions — [Daryo 2021](https://daryo.uz/2021/10/07/jamiyat-bizni-oziga-ogay-sanaydi-eshitish-va-gapirish-imkoniyati-cheklanganlar-ham-sifatli-talim-yaxshi-sharoit-izlab-toshkentga-kelishga-majbur-shifoko/)
- **Employment:**
  - Deaf men migrate to Tashkent for roadside selling and labour, and deaf women work as public weighers — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deafness_in_Uzbekistan)
  - Deaf theatre actors work as cleaners or taxi drivers — [Gazeta.uz 2026](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2026/09/29/theater-of-mimicry-and-gesture/)
  - "If conditions are created, we will have hokims and deputies too" (2018) — [Daryo 2018](https://daryo.uz/2018/01/05/sharoit-yaratib-berilsa-bizdan-ham-hokimu-deputatlar-chiqadi-eshitish-qobiliyatini-yoqotgan-ozbekistonliklarni-nimalar-qiynamoqda/)
- **Fairness:** a deaf man with Group II disability says paying for interpretation is unfair, since sign language is his native language — [Gazeta.uz 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)

### Inferences
- Priority use cases, ranked by evidence density:
  1. on-demand video interpreting (bank, hospital, state reception, police, notary);
  2. reliable emergency text and video contact;
  3. live Uzbek captions for lectures and TV;
  4. an interpreter booking directory;
  5. sign-language-first UI for people with low literacy.
- Willingness to pay among deaf individuals is low (they consider paying unfair), so the model should lean toward B2G and B2B payers (state bodies obliged under O'RQ-641, banks, clinics) and donors.

### Gaps
- No systematic media survey of 2024–2026 complaints (kun.uz, spot.uz, podrobno.uz) on specific banks, hospitals or notaries was done within the tool budget.
- No quantitative data on deaf employment or unemployment rates in Uzbekistan was found.
