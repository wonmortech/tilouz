# Non-profit and government sign-language / Deaf public-good platforms: models, funding, governance (as of 2026-11-15)

Scope note: research budget was limited (~19 tool calls). Many funders in the brief could not be checked; they are listed under Gaps rather than guessed. Facts below are from the cited pages; anything without a source is labeled as an inference.

## 1. Case studies with numbers (budget, team, users, years)

### Takeaway
Long-lived public sign-language platforms almost all run on one of three engines: (a) a government contract or line item (Contact Scotland, ISLRTC, VLibras), (b) repeated short research or EU project grants (Spreadthesign, Signbank-type corpora, K-RSL), or (c) volunteer or crowdsourced contribution on top of a small core (Be My Eyes, Lingua Libre). Published budget figures are rare. The best hard numbers found are Contact Scotland (about GBP 0.8-1.1M per year) and Spreadthesign's EU project budgets (EUR 45k-402k per project).

### Cited Findings

- Funded by the Scottish Government since 2014. Annual funding rose from about GBP 254,200 (2016/17) to GBP 282,215 (2018/19), with GBP 372,580 allocated for 2019/20. — [gov.scot FOI 19-01345](https://gov.scot/publications/foi-19-01345)
- Contract costs: 2022/23 GBP 1,139,544 total (core GBP 596,525 + extra minutes GBP 542,036, includes VAT recharge); 2023/24 GBP 863,447 (core GBP 324,720 + extra minutes GBP 541,614); 2024/25 GBP 796,369 (core GBP 324,720 + extra minutes GBP 471,649). The release gives no call or minute volumes. (The 2023/24 components sum to GBP 867,447, a GBP 4,000 mismatch with the stated total.) — [gov.scot FOI 202500459975](https://www.gov.scot/publications/foi-202500459975/)
- Re-tendered in 2025 as a "bundled monthly package" plus capped extra minutes (extra minutes not to exceed 50% of the bundled amount per year). — [Find a Tender notice 058414-2025](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/058414-2025)
- From 1 December 2025 the service is run by Sign Solutions, a private contractor. — [Orkney Islands Council](https://orkney.gov.uk/online/web-guide/accessibility/contactscotland-bsl)

**VLibras (Brazil: government + university, free open-source Libras translator)**
- Built by the LAViD lab at UFPB (Federal University of Paraiba). Equipment and materials were paid for by the Ministry of Planning and RNP (the national research network), with help from the Human Rights Secretariat. RNP turned the academic prototype into a national suite. — [RNP](https://rnp.br/en/news/digital-accessibility-official-version-suite-vlibras-available-deaf); [InfoEscola](https://www.infoescola.com/noticias/ministerio-do-planejamento-em-parceria-coma-ufpb-lanca-programa-que-traduz-conteudos-para-linguagem-brasileira-de-sinais/); [Teletime 2015](https://teletime.com.br/06/05/2015/tradutor-de-conteudos-digitais-para-surdos-e-lancado-pelo-governo/)
- Staffing is done through numbered public calls (editais) for paid researchers and students, for example "Fase 7" calls and a 2025 researcher call that includes 3D avatar development. — [UFPB edital 01/2025](https://progep.ufpb.br/ci/noticias/lavid-ufpb-abre-processo-seletivo-para-pesquisadores-e-estudantes-do-vlibras/Edital_VLIBRAS__01_2025__Pesquisadores.pdf); [UFPB edital 04/2024](https://ci.ufpb.br/ci/noticias/lavid-divulga-editais-para-pesquisadores-e-estudantes-do-projeto-vlibras/Edital_VLIBRAS04_2024__Estudantes.pdf)
- Usage: one undated article cites more than 10,000 app downloads and about 1,000 daily web visits. These figures are probably old. — [Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/vida-e-cidadania/aplicativo-para-deficientes-auditivos-transforma-conteudos-da-internet-em-libras-e5ofz3d3otvw255lyiz1r272w/)
- A Fundacao Banco do Brasil social-technology record links a TCU (federal audit court) evaluation and notes that VLibras lacked political and institutional sustainability. The date of that assessment is not clear. — [FBB Transforma](https://transforma.fbb.org.br/storage/socialtecnologies/482/files/Avaliacao_Vlibras_TCU.pdf)

**ISLRTC (India, state institute)**
- The ISL dictionary grew from 3,000 terms (1st ed., March 2018) to 6,000 (2nd ed., Feb 2019) to 10,000 (3rd ed., Feb 2021). — [Hindustan Times via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/india/hindustan-times-st-noida/20210219/282140704088781); [Outlook India](https://outlookindia.com/national/from-just-3-000-to-10-000-words-now-indian-sign-language-dictionary-grows-to-help-hearing-impaired-news-322677)
- In Sept 2023 it launched an online ISL self-learning course and released 260 financial-term signs made with a private partner (Societe Generale + V-Shesh). This shows a state institute co-producing domain vocabularies with corporate CSR money. — [Onmanorama](https://www.onmanorama.com/career-and-campus/top-news/2023/09/24/online-isl-course-260-new-financial-terms-signs-introduced.html)

Porto sigarra](https://sigarra.up.pt/fcup/en/PROJECTOS_GERAL.MOSTRA_PROJECTO?p_id=59915); [CIIE Porto](https://ciie.fpce.up.pt/en/project/sign-language-for-beginners-in-europe-spread-the-sign); [Ca' Foscari SPREADACS sheet](https://www.unive.it/pag/fileadmin/user_upload/ateneo/internazionale/europrogettazione_didattica/schede_progetti_finanziati/EKA2-SPREADACS-Cardinaletti.pdf); [IPS SPREADACS](https://investigacao.ips.pt/en/research/projects/spreadacs-vocational-accessibility-for-the-deaf-and-deafblind-with-spreadthesign)
- The site says it has documented over 400,000 signs. — [spreadthesign.com](https://spreadthesign.com/en.au/search/?cls=1)

**K-RSL (Kazakh-Russian Sign Language, Nazarbayev University)**
- Funded by an NU Faculty Development Competitive Research Grant, 2019-2021, "Kazakh Sign Language Automatic Recognition System (K-SLARS)", award 110119FD4545 (36 months). — [K-RSL project page](https://krslproject.github.io/krsl20); [NU news](https://nu.edu.kz/?p=3127)
- Corpus: 10 signers, 28,250 videos, 600 signs. A separate "online school" corpus was built from pandemic TV lessons: 7 interpreters, about 890 hours of cleaned video. Data collection started in 2015. — [LREC/SignLang 2022 paper](https://aclweb.org/anthology/2022.signlang-1.24.pdf); [K-RSL online school](https://krslproject.github.io/online-school); [SignLang 2024 retrospective](https://aclanthology.org/2024.signlang-1.12.pdf)
- FluentSigners-50 (crowdsourced K-RSL dataset) was also funded through the NU Faculty Development program. — [PLOS ONE / BORA](https://bora.uib.no/bora-xmlui/bitstream/11250/3037546/1/journal.pone.0273649.pdf); [NU research portal](https://research.nu.edu.kz/en/publications/crowdsourcing-kazakh-russian-sign-language-fluentsigners-50/)

**Cboard (open-source AAC, UNICEF Innovation Fund)**
- Cireha (the organization behind Cboard) joined the UNICEF Innovation Fund portfolio in Dec 2017, had about 15 months of investment, and graduated in 2019. It then got a second investment through the UNICEF CryptoFund in 2020. That cohort received "up to USD 100,000" each; Cboard's exact amount was not found. — [UNICEF Venture Fund](https://www.unicefventurefund.org/story/cireha-augmentative-and-alternative-communication); [Cboard blog 2019](https://www.cboard.io/en/blog/2019-05-15-unicef-innovation-fund-graduation/); [Cboard blog 2020](https://www.cboard.io/en/blog/2020-07-05-cboard-receives-another-UNICEF-investment/); [UNICEF ECA case study](https://www.unicef.org/eca/media/22051/file/Case%20study%201.pdf)

**Be My Eyes (volunteer model)**
- Jan 2025: more than 750,000 blind and low-vision users and more than 8.3 million sighted volunteers. March 2026: 1 million users and 10 million volunteers, plus a foundation launched to keep the AI tools free. — [Be My Eyes 10-year post](https://www.bemyeyes.com/?p=1003); [DoubleTap](https://doubletaponair.com/be-my-eyes-surpasses-1-million-users-and-launches-foundation-to-keep-ai-tools-free/)

**Hand Talk (Brazil, for-profit social-impact company)**
- Hand Talk uses avatars Hugo (2012) and Maya (2020) to translate Portuguese to Libras and English to ASL. It sells a website plugin to companies and says it has translated nearly 2 billion words. It says many deaf people work there (company claim). No funding data found. — [Hand Talk](https://www.handtalk.me/en/); [Hand Talk plugin](https://acessibilidade.handtalk.me/plugin-asl)

**Wikimedia-style: Lingua Libre / SignIt**
- Lingua Libre records audio and signed video. Content is CC BY-SA 4.0 and uploads go to Wikimedia Commons. The SignIt browser add-on (code MIT) shows LSF videos for French words: 660 words as of 2022. It also put out a 2024-2025 call for volunteer signers. — [Lingua Libre](https://lingualibre.org); [Meta: SignIt](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/SignIt); [WikiConference 2023](https://wikiconference.org/wiki/Submissions:2023/Sign_Languages_of_the_world_:_increase_mutual_understanding_with_Lingualibre_SignIt.)
- The LSF work began in 2017 through Wikimedia France with the association Signes de Sens, which said it was prepared to release its Elix dictionary videos under CC BY-SA. — [Meta: Wikimedia France LSF group](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wikimedia_France/Groupes_de_travail/Groupes_th%C3%A9matiques/Langues/LSF)

**Uzbekistan precedent**
- The Bukhara branch of the Uzbekistan Society of the Deaf ran s-access.uz (launched Aug 2025) under a UN small-grants programme. It is part of the EU/UNDP "Strengthening the national anti-corruption ecosystem" project. It has 48 anti-corruption terms in sign language plus video lessons; about 100 deaf people were trained and campaigns reached more than 5,000 people. — [EEAS Uzbekistan](https://eeas.europa.eu/delegations/uzbekistan/o%CA%BBzbekistonda-karlar-jamiyati-uchun-korrupsiyaga-qarshi-kurashish-bo%CA%BByicha-inklyuziv-onlayn_uz); [UNDP Uzbekistan](https://www.undp.org/uz/node/537801)

### Inferences
- Contact Scotland suggests a full national video relay service costs roughly GBP 0.8-1.1M per year in a high-income country. That is far outside a self-funded NGO's reach, so a relay or live-interpreter service only makes sense later and with state money. A dictionary/learning platform (Spreadthesign, ISLRTC, K-RSL scale) is the realistic first product.
- K-RSL is the closest regional analogue: one university research grant produced a 600-sign, 28k-video corpus. An Uzbek project could partner with a local university (for example a Tashkent IT/pedagogy university) to tap research grants instead of paying for everything itself.
- The s-access.uz project shows that EU/UNDP small grants already flow to the Uzbek Society of the Deaf. Partnering with it is the most direct, proven channel.
- A Be My Eyes-style "deaf volunteer interpreter" network is not directly transferable. Sighted volunteers are plentiful, but qualified sign-language interpreters are scarce, and WFD/WASLI stress quality (see section 4). A volunteer model fits low-stakes tasks better: sign recordings, validation, peer Q&A.

### Gaps
- No budgets or team sizes found for VLibras, ISLRTC, Hand Talk, or Signbank (Auslan/NZSL/others). Signbank funding (research grants) was not checked.
- No information on a Mozilla Common Voice equivalent for sign language with a CC0 license; only Lingua Libre (CC BY-SA) was found.
- Wikisign was not researched.

## 2. Funding sources open to an Uzbek non-profit open-source project (2026)

### Takeaway
The most realistic near-term money is local: Uzbekistan's state grant tenders for civil society institutions (about 130M so'm average per winner in the second 2026 round) and EU/UNDP small grants channelled via local NGOs. UNICEF Venture Fund requires open source but only funds for-profit companies in narrow themes. NLnet's NGI Zero Commons Fund is closed (final call 1 June 2026). Microsoft AI for Accessibility was a fixed five-year USD 25M program and has no confirmed open call.

### Cited Findings
**Uzbekistan state grants (Jamoat fondi / Public Fund for Support of NGOs under Oliy Majlis)**
- Second 2026 state grant tender: 700 projects received, 88 civil-society institutions won, 11.6 billion so'm in total (about 130M so'm, roughly USD 10k, per winner on average; the per-grant range was not published in the snippet). — [UzA](https://uza.uz/ar/posts/fuqarolik-jamiyati-institutlari-tashabbuslarini-amalga-oshirishga-116-milliard-som-yonaltiriladi_925975)
- First 2026 tender: 943 projects submitted. — [UzA](https://uza.uz/en/posts/davlat-granti-tanlovi-goliblari-aniqlandi_883953)
- A third 2026 tender was announced on themes such as women's and children's rights, gender equality, anti-corruption, environment, and public oversight in education and health. Disability or sign language was not named in the snippet. — [UzA](https://uza.uz/en/posts/davlat-granti-tanlovi-goliblari-aniqlandi_883953)
- Separate anti-corruption NGO grants: 10 billion so'm per year (2025 report). — [Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/news/2025/09/24/korrupsiyaga-qarshi-kurash-sohasidagi-nnt-loyihalariga-grant-ajratiladi)
- Applications portal: ngo.gov.uz; fund site: fundngo.uz (from search summary; not opened).

**EU / UNDP in Uzbekistan**
- An EU/UNDP anti-corruption project supported 8 CSOs in Bukhara, Fergana and Tashkent in 2024 through small grants; the Society of the Deaf's Bukhara branch was one of them. — [EEAS](https://eeas.europa.eu/delegations/uzbekistan/o%CA%BBzbekistonda-karlar-jamiyati-uchun-korrupsiyaga-qarshi-kurashish-bo%CA%BByicha-inklyuziv-onlayn_uz)

**UNICEF Venture Fund**
- Up to USD 100,000 equity-free. Applicants need a working prototype and must commit to open-source licensing. The focus through 2030 is blockchain, climate and femtech. Applicants must generally be registered private for-profit companies in a UNICEF programme country. 2026 calls: Climate & child health (deadline 17 May 2026; the official page still showed "rolling" at search time, so the sources conflict) and Blockchain (closed 10 March 2026). — [UNICEF climate call](https://www.unicef.org/innovation/call-for-application-climate-and-health-2026); [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/03/03/unicef-opens-applications-for-100000-equity-free-funding-for-early-stage-startups/); [UNICEF blockchain](https://www.unicef.org/innovation/equity-free-funding-blockchain-solutions); [VC4A](https://vc4a.com/unicef/unicef-venture-fund/)

**Digital Public Goods Alliance (DPGA)**
- The DPG Standard has 9 indicators: open licensing, open standards, platform independence, data privacy and security, legal compliance, do-no-harm by design, and others. Recognized DPGs are listed in the DPG Registry, which governments, NGOs and the private sector use. Benefits are discoverability, promotion and deployment opportunities, and the registry feeds DIAL's Catalog of Digital Solutions. The registry had 241 verified DPGs at the start of 2026. Registration is free (no grant). — [DPGA](https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/digital-public-goods); [DPG FAQ](https://digitalpublicgoods.net/digital-public-goods/faqs); [arXiv 2607.03427](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.03427)

Separate NGI Taler/Fediversity calls had a deadline of 1 August 2026. The "European dimension" eligibility requirement for non-EU applicants could not be confirmed. **Status: CLOSED.** — [NLnet Commons Fund](https://www.nlnet.nl/commonsfund); [NLnet news 2026-06](https://nlnet.nl/news/2026/20260612-NGIZero-stocktaking.html); [NLnet call 2026-06-01](https://nlnet.nl/news/2026/20260601-call.html)

**Microsoft AI for Accessibility**
- Launched as a USD 25M, five-year program (2018), which implies it was planned to end around 2023. The official page still describes grants and "five years of progress", but no current open call was confirmed. **Status: likely CLOSED or dormant; verify by email ai4aques@microsoft.com.** — [Microsoft](https://microsoft.com/ai-for-accessibility); [Disability Scoop](https://www.disabilityscoop.com/?p=25062)

### Inferences
- The legal form matters for eligibility. UNICEF Venture Fund wants a for-profit company, while Jamoat fondi grants want a registered NNT. A common pattern is an NNT for grants and donations plus, optionally, a small LLC for UNICEF-type calls. This is an inference, not checked against UNICEF's exact rules.
- DPG registration is cheap and useful for credibility with UNICEF, UNDP and governments. It requires open licenses for code and content and a do-no-harm/privacy posture, which fits a consent-based sign video dataset.
- In Uzbekistan, 130M so'm is roughly enough for a recording campaign of about 1,000 signs if Deaf signers are paid local rates (see section 5), but not for a full development team.

## 3. Legal form in Uzbekistan and licensing (code vs sign-video data)

### Takeaway
Registration as an NNT with the Ministry of Justice is mandatory; unregistered NGO activity is fined (10-25 BCV). Disability organizations get state-duty relief (halved from 1 Jan 2025; waived for some public associations under the Aug 2025 decree). For licensing, the cautionary model is ASL Citizen: open in spirit but non-commercial research only, with broad perpetual contributor licenses. Lingua Libre's CC BY-SA 4.0 is the main truly open precedent for sign video.

### Cited Findings
- NGO registration is mandatory via the Ministry of Justice, which may deny or delay applications. Activity of unregistered groups is prohibited. — [ICNL Civic Freedom Monitor](https://www.icnl.org/resources/civic-freedom-monitor/uzbekistan)
- August 2025 decree: re-registration time cut from one month to 15 days. No state fee for some public associations, including those of persons with disabilities, veterans, the elderly, women and children. — [Tashkent Times](https://www.tashkenttimes.uz/national/6528-mirziyoyev-signs-decree-banning-government-interference-into-ngos-activities)
- From 1 Jan 2025 the state duty for registering organizations of persons with disabilities, veterans, women and children was halved. Territorial NGOs pay 50% of the BCV. — [Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2024/08/26/uzbekistan-introduces-tax-benefits-and-new-platforms-for-civil-society-support)
- Fines for operating without registration: 10-25 BCV. — [UzDaily](https://uzdaily.ru/en/president-signs-law-regulating-ngos-and-introducing-new-fines)
- Foreign grants are governed by Regulation No. 527 (2023), with a threshold of 100 BCV per year tied to MoJ procedures. ICNL says registration remains burdensome despite the reforms. — [ICNL overview of Regulation 527](https://www.icnl.org/wp-content/uploads/Overview_Uzbekistan-Regulation-527-Oct-2023_FV.pdf); [ICNL](https://www.icnl.org/resources/civic-freedom-monitor/uzbekistan)
- Jan 2026: new criteria for "publicly important organizations" and simplified financial reporting. — [Daryo](https://daryo.uz/en/2026/01/07/uzbekistan-sets-new-criteria-for-publicly-important-organizations-and-simplifies-financial-reporting/)
- ASL Citizen (Microsoft Research): 83,399 videos, 2,731 signs, 52 signers, collected under IRB with consent. The dataset license allows only non-commercial, non-revenue research, and users must destroy personal data after research. The contributor consent form grants Microsoft a broad, perpetual, irrevocable license and says data already released cannot be deleted. — [ASL Citizen license](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/asl-citizen/dataset-license/); [consent form](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/asl-citizen/consent-form/); [arXiv 2304.05934](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.05934v2)
- Lingua Libre: content CC BY-SA 4.0, videos on Wikimedia Commons; SignIt code MIT, with video licensing kept separate from the code license. — [Lingua Libre](https://lingualibre.org); [Meta: SignIt](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/SignIt)

### Inferences
- Recommended split (inference): code under a permissive or copyleft OSI license (Apache-2.0 for patent grant, or AGPL-3.0 to stop closed SaaS forks), and sign videos under CC BY-SA 4.0 (Lingua Libre precedent, DPG-compatible) or CC BY 4.0. CC0 maximizes reuse but gives signers no attribution. Face video is biometric personal data, so consent must cover AI-training reuse explicitly and be layered (dictionary display / open dataset / ML training).
- ASL Citizen's "no deletion after release" clause is the honest reality of open datasets. An Uzbek project should state it plainly in Uzbek and Russian, and in sign-language video, at consent time.
- A partnership with the Uzbekistan Society of the Deaf can give legitimacy and access to its existing grant track record. The fee waivers apply to disability public associations, so the project might register as one if Deaf people are founders or members. This needs legal confirmation on lex.uz.

### Gaps
- The NNT law text on lex.uz, the difference between a foundation ("jamoat fondi") and a public association, the exact state duty in so'm, and whether a non-disabled founder can register a disability association were not checked.
- Common Voice's CC0 consent wording was not retrieved.

## 4. Governance: Deaf-led control, fair pay, avoiding the "hearing-led tech" trap

### Takeaway
The only formal international guidance found is the 2018 WFD-WASLI statement on signing avatars. It opposes replacing human signers with avatars or machine translation, especially for live, complex or high-stakes content, and says sign language work should be led by Deaf people. No WFD document specifically on Deaf-led technology governance or contributor pay was found.

### Cited Findings
- WFD and WASLI (2018): avatars and machine translation do not match qualified human interpreters. Sign languages have their own grammar, so word-to-sign substitution fails. The highest concern is avatars for live, complex or high-stakes content such as news. — [WFD-WASLI statement PDF](https://wfdeaf.org/wp-content/uploads/WFD-and-WASLI-Statement-on-Avatar-FINAL-14032018-Updated-14042018.pdf); [WASLI](https://wasli.org/news/wfd-wasli-statement-use-signing-avatars/)
- A 2021 W3C accessibility discussion followed this stance and preferred pre-recorded video by qualified interpreters, while treating avatars as an emerging area. This was a draft discussion, not settled guidance. — [W3C RQTF list](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-rqtf/2021May/0028.html)
- Hand Talk presents inclusion of deaf staff as part of its model (self-reported). — [Hand Talk](https://www.handtalk.me/en/)

### Inferences
- Practical governance pattern (synthesized, not from one source): (1) a majority-Deaf board or a Deaf co-founder with veto on language content; (2) a Deaf community advisory council drawn from regional Society of the Deaf branches (Uzbek, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, Tajik, Russian sign variants); (3) sign data owned or stewarded by the NNT under open licenses, never sold; (4) every recorded signer paid per session, not unpaid "volunteering"; (5) avatar or AI output clearly labelled and never used for high-stakes content, in line with WFD-WASLI.
- The "hearing-led tech" trap: VLibras' institutional fragility and avatar-first products (Hand Talk) are the very things WFD-WASLI warn about. A human-video-first dictionary is safer for legitimacy.

### Gaps
- WFD's newer documents (for example on AI or the Charter on Sign Language Rights) and any guidance on paying Deaf consultants were not retrieved.
- No published fair-pay rate cards for Deaf signers in CIS countries were found.

## 5. Cost benchmarks

### Takeaway
Hard benchmarks found are limited to high-income relay contracts (Contact Scotland about GBP 0.8-1.1M per year) and EU project budgets (EUR 45k-400k per multi-year Spreadthesign project). Data on regional interpreter rates, video hosting at scale, and per-sign recording cost could not be found within budget.

### Cited Findings
- Contact Scotland BSL relay: GBP 796,369 (2024/25), with "extra minutes" making up more than half of the cost in 2023/24 and 2024/25. — [gov.scot FOI](https://www.gov.scot/publications/foi-202500459975/)
- Spreadthesign EU projects: EUR 402,313 (2006-08 pilot), EUR 45,479 (2012-15), EUR 400,000 (SPREADACS 2025-28). — [Univ. Porto](https://sigarra.up.pt/fcup/en/PROJECTOS_GERAL.MOSTRA_PROJECTO?p_id=59915); [Ca' Foscari](https://www.unive.it/pag/fileadmin/user_upload/ateneo/internazionale/europrogettazione_didattica/schede_progetti_finanziati/EKA2-SPREADACS-Cardinaletti.pdf)
- Scale reference: K-RSL produced 28,250 videos of 600 signs from 10 signers (about 47 repetitions per sign) on one university grant. ASL Citizen gathered 83,399 videos of 2,731 signs from 52 signers. — [K-RSL 2022](https://aclweb.org/anthology/2022.signlang-1.24.pdf); [ASL Citizen](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/asl-citizen/)
- Uzbek grant sizing: about 130M so'm average per state-grant winner (2026, round 2). — [UzA](https://uza.uz/ar/posts/fuqarolik-jamiyati-institutlari-tashabbuslarini-amalga-oshirishga-116-milliard-som-yonaltiriladi_925975)

### Inferences
- A dictionary (one reference video per sign, about 1,000-2,000 signs) is a much smaller job than an ML dataset (dozens of repetitions per sign across many signers, as in K-RSL and ASL Citizen). Budget the two separately. Phase 1 (dictionary) could plausibly fit within one Jamoat fondi-size grant plus self-funding. This is an estimate, not sourced.
- Hosting: short sign clips (about 2-5 s, 720p, H.264/AV1) are small. A 2,000-sign dictionary is likely only a few GB, so storage is cheap and egress or CDN is the real cost driver. This is an inference; no sourced price was gathered.
