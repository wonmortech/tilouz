# Deaf-communication tech in South/Southeast Asia, MENA/Turkey/Iran and Africa (status Nov 2026)

Scope note: these notes go deeper than global_feature_matrix.md and nonprofit_models_funding.md. Those files already cover ISLRTC relay and SignAble in one line each, so they are not repeated here. About 28 searches were run. Many sources are app-store pages or news items. Where a page date looked unreliable (several 2026-dated South African pages describe old launches), this is flagged. "DEAD?" means no 2023-2026 activity was found, not that the project is confirmed closed.

## Q1. South Asia (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal)

### Takeaway
The strongest low-resource model in this region is a state or NGO **video dictionary of 5,000-10,000 signs** (ISLRTC 10,000; Deaf Reach PSL about 6,000-7,500), plus a **free relay that runs over WhatsApp video** (ISLRTC). Paid interpreter apps (DeafTawk, SignAble) survive on telecom partnerships and GSMA or Prosus grants. AI translation remains research or hype. AI4Bharat's INCLUDE (263 signs) and OpenHands (Apache-2.0) are the reusable open assets.

### Cited Findings
**India: state**
- ISLRTC dictionary: 10,000 terms in 6 categories, with videos and English/Hindi equivalents. It includes regional variants, such as numbers and colours from Hyderabad, Mumbai, Kolkata, Munger and Manipur. — [ISLRTC FAQ](https://islrtc.nic.in/faq/)
- Growth path: the first edition (March 2018) had 3,000 Hindi/English words. The 10,000-term edition and an online self-learning ISL course launched on International Day of Sign Languages, September 2023, along with 260 new signs for financial terms. — [NAD India](https://nadindia.org/article/4405/first-indian-sign-language-dictionary-of-3000-words-launched); [Onmanorama 2023-09-24](https://www.onmanorama.com/career-and-campus/top-news/2023/09/24/online-isl-course-260-new-financial-terms-signs-introduced.html)
- ISLRTC video relay: free online interpreting since 18 July 2023. — [ISLRTC Annual Report 2023-24](https://divyangjan.depwd.gov.in/upload/s3wassupload/ISLRTC_New_Delhi_Annual_Report_2023_24.pdf). It is reported to run on **WhatsApp video calls** to remote interpreters, with no custom app. — [same search result set / annual report](https://divyangjan.depwd.gov.in/upload/s3wassupload/ISLRTC_New_Delhi_Annual_Report_2023_24.pdf)
- ISH News covered the SignLearn app (ISLRTC's dictionary app) as "a sign language dictionary for all". — [ISH News](https://ishnews.tv/videos/news/signlearn-app-a-sign-language-dictionary-for-all)
- Interpreter supply: "fewer than three hundred certified ISL interpreters" (startup-sourced figure, treat as indicative). — [NITI Frontier Tech](https://frontiertech.niti.gov.in/story/innovative-isl-technology-empowers-indias-deaf-community-through-accessible-education/)
- Bhashini: no official Bhashini sign-language module was found. One lightly sourced outlet claims an AI avatar rendered the PM's speech in ISL in real time at the India AI Summit 2026. This is unconfirmed. — [startupfeed.in](https://startupfeed.in/pm-modi-ai-sign-language-translation-at-india-ai-summit-2026/)

**India: open data and research**
- INCLUDE (AI4Bharat / IIT Madras): 4,287 videos, 0.27M frames, 263 word signs in 15 categories. INCLUDE-50 is a subset. Signers are deaf students from St. Louis School for the Deaf, Chennai. Hosted on Zenodo and HF. The card says it was made "for education and ISLR, but may be used for other purposes". **No explicit data licence was found.** — [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/4010759); [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/INCLUDE); [GitHub](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/INCLUDE)
- OpenHands (AI4Bharat): pose-based pretrained ISLR models across languages, **Apache-2.0**. The README says to check each dataset's licence. A third party reports it is no longer actively maintained. — [GitHub OpenHands](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/OpenHands); [ecosyste.ms](https://science.ecosyste.ms/projects/12174)

**India: startups and Deaf-led media**
- SignAble Communications (Bengaluru, founder Rupmani Chhetri): on-demand ISL interpretation app. Funded by the GSMA Innovation Fund (to build a two-way calling app) and the Prosus Social Impact Challenge for Accessibility. Voice of SAP subsidises 11 months of subscription for users. — [GSMA grantee page](https://www.gsma.com/mobilefordevelopment/digital-grantees-portfolio/signable-communications/); [GSMA blog](https://www.gsma.com/mobilefordevelopment/blog/making-sign-language-interpretation-accessible-for-all-through-mobile-assistive-technology/); [VOSAP](https://www.voiceofsap.org/?p=139887); [Better India](https://thebetterindia.com/367935/signable-app-for-deaf-people-rupmani-chhetri-startup-bengaluru)
- ISH News (India Signing Hands, Deaf-led, launched Nov 2018 by Alok Kejriwal and Aqil Chinoy, Gallaudet alumni): daily ISL news. 449,000+ YouTube subscribers and about 202M views across FB/YT/IG (2025 profile). Interpreted a Bollywood film into ISL. — [Gallaudet](https://gallaudet.edu/alumni-relations/alumni-in-india-make-bollywood-film-accessible-in-indian-sign-language/); [The Core](https://www.thecore.in/business/can-indian-media-make-accessibility-a-business-570830)
- Bleetech Innovations (Pune): BleeTV, a free Android app and web portal of ISL content. 12,000+ users in 15 states. — [NITI Frontier Tech](https://frontiertech.niti.gov.in/story/innovative-isl-technology-empowers-indias-deaf-community-through-accessible-education/)
- Other 2025 ISL startups: DeepVisionTech.AI "Let'sTalkSign" (speech/text to sign and back) [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/05/deepvisiontech-ai-letstalksign-accessibility-startup); D.I.T.U (ISL wearable glove) [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/06/sign-language-gesture-tech-ditu-startup); SignSetu (Deaf founder Shraddha Agarwal, bilingual ISL-English literacy for children) [Better India](https://thebetterindia.com/changemakers/shraddha-agarwal-signsetu-deaf-literacy-isl-english-learning-platform-for-children-10943790)
- Not found: "Talking Hands", "Sign Together", "DeafCan", "Hear Me" (India) and "Deaf Enabled Foundation". There were no search hits. NIEPMD and telecom relay were not researched.

**Pakistan**
- PSL by Deaf Reach (Family Educational Services Foundation, FESF, an NGO): free iOS and Android app. Each sign video has spoken and written English and Urdu. — [App Store id1619629318](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1619629318)
- Dictionary size varies by source: "6,000 word PSL dictionary" plus animated stories, teacher tutorials, classroom demos and IQRA literacy (psl.org.pk); "7,500+ signs documented and digitized" (FESF programs page); "7,000 words" (WISE Qatar). — [FESF programs](https://fesf.org.pk/programs/); [WISE](https://wise-qatar.org/?p=13930); [psl.org.pk resources (search snippet; site DNS failed on fetch)](https://new.psl.org.pk/resources)
- Pattern: an NGO school network (Deaf Reach schools) produced the dictionary as a by-product of teaching. Winning a WISE (Qatar Foundation) award brought money and visibility. — [WISE](https://wise-qatar.org/?p=13930)
- PSL SignBank (academic): a machine-readable multimodal dictionary of only 300 words. — [CORE](https://core.ac.uk/outputs/739512648)
- DeafTawk (startup, 2018): paid on-demand interpreting app. Takes a 20% commission and pays the interpreter 80%. Prices are set by regional unit economics. GSMA reports about 12,900 B2B and about 208,800 B2C customers (undated). Received a $250k GSMA Innovation Fund grant (July 2021) and backing from Jazz and MoITT. A GSMA 2026 case study (data collected Dec 2025-Mar 2026) exists. — [GSMA DeafTawk](https://www.gsma.com/mobilefordevelopment/digital-grantees-portfolio/triac-deaftawk/); [GSMA 2023 PDF](https://www.gsma.com/get-involved/gsma-foundry/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/DeafTawk-Helping-The-Deaf-To-Communicate.pdf); [GSMA 2026 PDF](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/GSMA_Pakistan-Market-Report—DeafTawk-Case-Study-final.pdf)
- The Deaf Reach to imo-ishora.uz (Uzbekistan) partnership could **not** be verified. No search hits connected them. — see Gaps.

**Sri Lanka**
- Dialog (telecom) localised DeafTawk in Sri Lanka. It needs internet plus purchased talk time, and DeafTawk is piloting a licensing model there. — [Dialog](https://dialog.lk/apps/deaftawk-app); [GSMA 2026 PDF](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/GSMA_Pakistan-Market-Report—DeafTawk-Case-Study-final.pdf)
- A University of Colombo thesis (April 2025) notes "few assistive tools accessible" for Deaf people in Sri Lanka. — [UCSC](https://dl.ucsc.cmb.ac.lk/jspui/bitstream/123456789/4957/1/20020155%2c%2020020262%2c%2020021011%20.pdf)

**Nepal**
- Mero Sanket: a free, offline Nepali Sign Language learning app, the first in Nepal. Built by Humanity & Inclusion with the National Federation of the Deaf Nepal under a USAID-supported programme. It has 6 lessons (vowels, consonants, words, punctuation). A companion app, Mero Sati, teaches reading with signs. — [HI-US](https://www.hi-us.org/en/news/sign-language-app-opens-new-path-to-learning-for-deaf-children-in-nepal)

**Bangladesh**
- UNDP Bangladesh launched an open-access digital Bangla Sign Language (BdSL) dictionary. — [Green Network Asia](https://greennetwork.asia/news/improving-access-to-bangla-sign-language-learning-through-digital-dictionary)
- First mental-health sign bank for Deaf people, based on a Lancet Psychiatry paper (Monash co-design). — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/bangladesh/397898/bangladesh-launches-first-mental-health-sign); [Monash](https://www.monash.edu/mats/projects/co-designing-digital-mental-health-interventions)
- "Love to Learn Sign Language": a free iOS BdSL guide that works offline. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6757427283)

### Inferences
- The ISLRTC relay shows that a relay can start with **WhatsApp video and a duty roster of interpreters**, with no custom VRS stack. That is directly copyable for an Uzbek NGO.
- The ISLRTC and Deaf Reach dictionaries grew in stages: 2,500-3,000 core signs first, then domain packs (finance, health). An Uzbek MVP should aim for about 3,000 signs and then add domain packs.
- The paid relay model (DeafTawk 80/20 split) works only with telecom distribution and grants. A non-profit would fund interpreter time from grants (GSMA Innovation Fund, Prosus, WISE) and keep the software free and open.
- Deaf-led media (ISH News) produces the most reach per dollar. A content channel is a cheap community-growth lever.

### Gaps
- ISLRTC relay call volumes, interpreter count and wait times were not found in any public source checked.
- INCLUDE video licence is unspecified. Check the Zenodo record and contact AI4Bharat before reuse.
- Deaf Reach dataset licence: no open licence was found, and the site was unreachable (DNS failure). The current sign count is uncertain (6,000 / 7,000 / 7,500).
- The Deaf Reach to imo-ishora.uz partnership is unverified.
- Bhashini sign features: no primary source was found.
- Not found: Talking Hands, Sign Together, DeafCan, Hear Me India, Deaf Enabled Foundation, NIEPMD tools, Indian telecom relay.

## Q2. Southeast Asia (Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Vietnam, Singapore)

### Takeaway
Thailand's **TTRS** is the region's flagship state relay: voice, text and video, with public kiosks, funded through the telecom regulator's USO levy. It shows the core risk of state relays: it was **suspended in June 2025 over NBTC funding delays**, and no confirmed restart was found. Elsewhere the landscape is mostly university prototypes (BISINDO, BIM) and telecom CSR apps (Digi "Eddy").

### Cited Findings
- TTRS (Thailand): multi-mode relay (voice, text, video) for Deaf, hard-of-hearing and speech-impaired users. Free app plus public video-relay kiosks. — [NBTC TTRS page](https://nbtc.go.th/Business/commu/telecom/บริการ-USO/TTRS/test.aspx?lang=en-us); [The Story Thailand](https://www.thestorythailand.com/en/28/04/2021/20524/)
- Scale: about 40,000 subscribed Deaf users, of whom about 10,000+ use it regularly. Thailand has about 500 sign interpreters, about 40 of them working for TTRS. — [Thai PBS World](https://www.thaipbsworld.com/science-technology/the-impact-of-technology-on-the-hard-of-hearing). An older figure gives about 160,000 sign-video calls per year. — [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/tech/247700/the-new-way-to-talk). The relay saw a 250% increase in use (date unclear). — [nwise](https://nwise.se/?p=1237)
- Funding: project-based USO money from NBTC, whose budget comes from a 2.5% levy on telecom revenue. — [Prachatai](https://prachataienglish.com/node/11567)
- Suspension: TTRS announced a hiatus on 13 June 2025 because NBTC funding had lapsed. The Deaf community protested at NBTC on 4 July. — [Magzter/Bangkok Post](https://www.magzter.com/en/stories/newspaper/Bangkok-Post/COMMUNICATION-BREAKDOWN); [Prachatai](https://prachataienglish.com/node/11567); [CMKL](https://cmkl.ac.th/projects-research/deaf-ai-translator). In July and September 2026, Kasetsart University and the national rights commission published a study recommending that TRS/TTRS become a basic right. — [Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/1237978). TTRS was a WSIS Prizes 2025 project entry. — [ITU WSIS](https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/stocktaking/Prizes/2025/Details/15435411781736025)
- CMKL University (Bangkok) is building a "Deaf AI translator" explicitly as a response to the TTRS suspension. — [CMKL](https://cmkl.ac.th/projects-research/deaf-ai-translator)
- Thai Sign Language digital dictionary (Kasetsart, 2007 research project). It notes regional ThSL varieties. Status: legacy. — [KU ThSL](https://pirun.ku.ac.th/~fhumalt/THSL/new_ThSL%20Digital%20Dict_23%20%20May%202007/Eng/preface.htm)
- Malaysia: DITE (Deaf In Touch Everywhere) app from Monash Malaysia's HEARD project offers interpreter video calls plus a BIM medical-term dictionary. — [Monash HEARD](https://www.monash.edu/mats/projects/improving-healthcare-access). Digi (telecom) launched the "Eddy" BIM learning app in 2017, with signs checked by the Malaysian Federation of the Deaf (status DEAD?). — [The Star 2017](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2017/12/21/digi-launches-eddy-sign-language-mobile-app). "MyBIM" and "Tunjuk" were not found.
- Indonesia: BISINDO apps found are mostly 2023-2025 academic prototypes (TFLite alphabet recognisers, CNN web demos). — [USNI 2025](https://ojs-teknik.usni.ac.id/index.php/jsk/article/view/763); [WICIDA](https://repository.wicida.ac.id/6370/). BISINDO, not SIBI, is the language the Deaf community actually uses. — [Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2015/07/15/including-people-all-abilities-digital-revolution). "Hear Me" was not found.
- Philippines: only SLEC-D (Cebuano-English sign dictionary for hearing parents) was found. No Filipino Sign Language app or "SignBridge" surfaced. — [search result set](https://www.gadgetmatch.com/hearo-app-sign-language-translate-voice-text-apple-developer-academy/) (Hearo, an Apple Developer Academy student app, is also listed)
- Vietnam: "Adley", a 2026 AI app with 3 core functions for Deaf communication (competition stage). — [Saigoneer](https://saigoneer.com/saigon-technology/25807-what-creating-a-sign-language-app-taught-me-about-the-hardships-of-vietnam-s-deaf-community). A Vietnamese Sign Language SignPuddle dictionary was announced in 2018. — [SW-L list](https://listserv.linguistlist.org/pipermail/sw-l/2018-August/062147.html)
- Singapore: only a free SgSL e-book was found. No signbank was confirmed. — [theAsianparent](https://sg.theasianparent.com/singapore-sign-language)

### Inferences
- TTRS is the best state model to cite to Uzbek government (multi-mode, kiosks, about 40 interpreters serving about 10k regular users). Its 2025 collapse is also the strongest argument that **an NGO platform must not depend on a single state budget line**. Interpreter labour should be funded from diversified grants and the software kept open so it outlives any funding gap.
- The interpreter-to-user ratio (about 40 TTRS interpreters for about 10,000 regular users) is a usable planning benchmark.

### Gaps
- Whether TTRS restarted in 2026 is unconfirmed. Check ttrs.or.th or NBTC.
- No real usage data was found for any Indonesian, Malaysian, Filipino or Vietnamese app. FSL, MyBIM, Tunjuk and Hear Me were not found.

## Q3. Middle East, Turkey, Iran, Israel

### Takeaway
The Gulf states fund **state-built signing avatars and toolkits** (Qatar's Mada BuHamad and Jumla Sign Language SDK). Turkey has the largest state dictionaries: MEB's 5,000-word TID dictionary, with a 2,607-word first version in 2015, and the Family Ministry dictionary, with 4M+ accesses. Turkey also has the largest open research dataset (AUTSL, academic-only licence). Startups (Saudi Tawaasuly, Jordanian SignBook and MarthaEDU) are small and hard to verify.

### Cited Findings
**Qatar (Mada Assistive Technology Center, state)**
- BuHamad: a 3D Qatari Sign Language avatar, in development since 2019 and launched in 2021 as part of the "Jumla Sign Language" project. It translates website content into QSL. — [Mada Nafath paper](https://nafath.mada.org.qa/nafath-article/mcn-20-01/); [Mada launch news](https://mada.org.qa/mada-center-launches-the-first-qatari-virtual-3d-interpreter-for-qatari-sign-language/)
- Jumla Sign Language Kit: lets developers embed BuHamad on their own sites. — [Jumla services](https://jumla.mada.org.qa/services/?lang=en); [Mada session](https://mada.org.qa/session/jumla-sign-language-the-qatari-sign-language-processing-toolkit/)
- Deployed on the Ministry of Social Development and Family website. — [MSDF](https://www.msdf.gov.qa/en/media-center/news-list/he-minister-social-development-and-family-launches-avatar-bo-hamad-virtual)
- 2025-26: a standalone BuHamad site markets it as Qatar's first AI that turns text and speech into sign language, and as a government-service assistant. It shows promotional pricing (not verified). — [BuHamad site](https://buhamad.qatarcentral.cloudapp.azure.com/en/about)
- A user study found Deaf and hard-of-hearing participants in Qatar "predominantly favorable" toward the avatar. — [Mada Edge paper](https://edge.mada.org.qa/publications/paper40/)
- No open-source licence was found. It is a proprietary state toolkit.

**Saudi Arabia**
- Unified Saudi Sign Language Dictionary issued, with the Saudi Association for the Deaf leading unification. Regional variation within KSA is noted. — [PMC 2024 (citing Asharq Al-Awsat)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11124802)
- Tawasol app (built on Tawasol centre videos): dictionary, tutorial and fingerspelling editor. — [Springer](https://rd.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-20681-3_30)
- Tawaasuly (founder Reem Turky): Arabic speech and text to 3D-animated Arabic SL, and sign to text/speech. An enterprise version is planned (Oct 2024). — [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/2574932)
- Efhamni: academic deep-learning Saudi SL recognition app (Sensors, May 2024). — [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/05fe7269c37d488cafc9c023a2cb1c9e)

**Turkey**
- MEB (Ministry of Education) with TÜBİTAK: a 5,000-word video TID dictionary plus the TID Education Portal at tid.meb.gov.tr. The first version (2015) had 2,607 words, each signed by Deaf adults. — [TRT Haber](https://www.trthaber.com/haber/turkiye/isaret-dilini-ogrenmek-artik-daha-kolay-435005.html); [MEB](https://www.meb.gov.tr/isitme-engelli-ogrenciler-icin-dijital-egitim-platformu/haber/25652/tr)
- MEB digital education platform for Deaf students at orgm.meb.gov.tr/icdep, with textbooks. — [NTV](https://www.ntv.com.tr/egitim/meb-isitme-engelli-ogrenciler-icin-dijital-egitim-platformu-kurdu,_KbAQ0xUUkGzSt8see9j9w)
- Family and Social Services Ministry "Güncel Türk İşaret Dili Sözlüğü": online since 2017, compiled from 116 native signers, with 4M+ accesses from 150 countries. — [aile.gov.tr](https://aile.gov.tr/haberler/bakanligin-guncel-turk-isaret-dili-sozlugune-150-ulkeden-4-milyondan-fazla-erisim-saglandi/)
- e-Devlet "Engelsiz Çağrı": a state app giving Deaf citizens video support from the e-Government contact centre. The store-reported download count is tiny. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1584651862); [Tamindir](https://www.tamindir.com/indir/e-devlet-kapisi-engelsiz-cagri/)
- Engelsiz Çeviri (2016, Eskişehir, TÜBİTAK BİGG grant): a website plugin that rewrites and explains text for Deaf readers. It is not a video interpreter. No user numbers were found. — [Habertürk](https://www.haberturk.com/isitme-engelli-bireyler-engelsiz-ceviri-ile-okuduklarini-anlayacaklar-2739998)
- Turkcell "İşaret Dilim" (telecom CSR): translation, dictionary and lessons. — [Tamindir](https://www.tamindir.com/indir/isaret-dilim/). "Sesim Elim" is a TID learning and dictionary iOS app. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/sesim-elim/id1596642299?l=tr)
- Open-source community: a TID dictionary repo exists on GitHub. — [oncado86/Turkish_Sign_Language_Dictionary](https://github.com/oncado86/Turkish_Sign_Language_Dictionary)
- AUTSL dataset (Ankara University): 226 signs, 43 signers, 38,336 isolated videos, Kinect v2 RGB + depth + skeleton. **Licence: academic/research only, no commercial use.** — [arXiv 2008.00932](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.00932v2); [Hamburg catalogue](https://www.sign-lang.uni-hamburg.de/lrec/data/autsl.html)
- 112 emergency video in sign language: no source was found.

**Israel**
- Institute for the Advancement of Deaf Persons: online Israeli Sign Language dictionary (isl.org.il). Just over 3,000 clips, with glosses in Hebrew, English, Arabic and Russian. Also a "Signs in Crisis" app. — [Algemeiner](https://www.algemeiner.com/?p=292490); [Israel365](https://israel365news.com/?p=301948)
- IssieSign (Beit Issie Shapiro with SAP Labs Israel): free iOS ISL vocabulary app. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/issiesign/id1423489221)

**Jordan**
- SignBook: speech to sign via video call with an interpreter. — [Wamda 2017](https://www.wamda.com/en/2017/07/lost-translation-jordanian-startup-helping-deaf)
- MarthaEDU (Umniah telecom support): Arabic SL teaching videos for children aged 2-5. — [Umniah](https://www.umniah.com/?p=41543)
- RAMZ: a web sign interpreter for online text. 2013 university dictionary project (status DEAD?). — [Jordan Times](https://jordantimes.com/node/353421); [Wamda 2013](https://www.wamda.com/en/2013/07/meet-the-jordanian-students-workin-on-a-sign-language-dictionnary-wamda-tv)

**Iran, UAE, Egypt**: no app, relay or dataset surfaced in the searches.

### Inferences
- Turkey is the closest analogue to Uzbekistan: a Turkic spoken language, a strong state, and ministries that ran a dictionary from 2,607 to 5,000 words with TÜBİTAK. The 4M-access figure shows that a free, government-endorsed web dictionary gets wide reach. The Uzbek platform should seek Ministry of Preschool and School Education endorsement and a state research-agency partner.
- Qatar's Jumla kit shows that an avatar is most useful as an **embeddable SDK for government websites**, not as a consumer app. Avatar quality complaints are common elsewhere, so treat an avatar as phase 3, after dictionary and relay.
- AUTSL's non-commercial licence is fine for a non-profit's research, but it blocks any downstream commercial reuse. An Uzbek dataset should use CC BY 4.0 (or CC BY-NC with Deaf consent) to avoid that trap.

### Gaps
- Turkey's 112 video sign service, "Engelsiz Çeviri" usage, and the MEB dictionary's licence (likely closed) were not found.
- No Iranian (ZEI/Persian SL), Emirati or Egyptian projects were found. Mada tool usage numbers and BuHamad vocabulary size were not found.

## Q4. Africa (Kenya, South Africa, Ghana, Rwanda, Nigeria, Ethiopia, Uganda)

### Takeaway
Kenya is the most active hub. It has a pan-African, crowd-contributed dictionary app (DEP Dictionary, 5,000+ signs), an avatar startup that won the Africa Prize (Signvrse Terp 360, 2,300 recorded KSL signs), and a per-second paid interpreter app (assistALL by Signs Media). South Africa and Rwanda show the state-dictionary route: PanSALB's bilingual app, and Rwanda's printed dictionary of 2024 still awaiting digitisation. Open African datasets (AfriSign, CASL-W60, SignTalk-Gh) appeared in 2025, mostly under academic-only terms.

### Cited Findings
**Kenya**
- Signvrse "Terp 360": English/Swahili to KSL with 3D avatars. Recorded 2,300+ local signs with the Deaf community. About 2,000 web users (Aug 2025), with a mobile app planned for Dec 2025 (launch not confirmed). Won the 2025 Africa Prize for Engineering Innovation (GBP 50,000). Plans other African sign languages by mid-2027. A Deaf trainer criticised errors and said it "primarily benefits hearing users". — [The Star 2025-10-23](https://www.the-star.co.ke/health/2025-10-23-kenyan-creates-first-sign-language-app); [Citizen](https://www.citizen.digital/tech/kenyan-ai-app-turning-speech-into-sign-language-wins-african-award-n371770); [Africanews](https://www.africanews.com/2025/08/11/kenyan-startup-signvrse-pioneers-ai-powered-sign-language-translation/); [Techsoma](https://techsoma.africa/24-year-old-kenyan-creates-ai-sign-language-app-wins-67k-engineering-prize/)
- assistALL (Signs Media Kenya Ltd, the Signs TV group): described as Kenya's first mobile app for sign interpretation, billed per second. — [search snippet, Kenya News](https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/?p=184673)

### Inferences
- DEP Dictionary's **crowd-contribution by Deaf experts** and DEAFinition's **"request a word"** are cheap, proven features for an Uzbek dictionary MVP.
- PanSALB's handshape and body-location search is a must for a sign-to-word reverse lookup without AI.
- The Signvrse criticism repeats the global pattern: avatar and AI translation impresses funders, but Deaf users rate it poorly. Lead with human video and Deaf-signed content.
- Rwanda's 10-year print-first route is a warning. Build digital-first and publish incrementally.

## Q5. Cross-cutting lessons for an Uzbek non-profit open-source platform

### Takeaway
Every working low-resource deployment followed the same order: (1) a Deaf-signed **video dictionary of about 3,000 signs, growing to 10,000**, with domain packs; (2) **human interpreting over commodity video** (WhatsApp in India, a simple app in Thailand); (3) Deaf-led content. AI and avatars come last. Open licensing is rare: only OpenHands is Apache-2.0, and most datasets are academic-only. A CC BY Uzbek dataset would be distinctive.

### Cited Findings
- Dictionary sizes: ISLRTC 10,000 ([FAQ](https://islrtc.nic.in/faq/)); MEB TID 5,000 ([TRT](https://www.trthaber.com/haber/turkiye/isaret-dilini-ogrenmek-artik-daha-kolay-435005.html)); PSL 6,000-7,500 ([FESF](https://fesf.org.pk/programs/)); DEP 5,000+ ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1437104597)); Israel ISL about 3,000 ([Algemeiner](https://www.algemeiner.com/?p=292490)); Signvrse 2,300 ([The Star](https://www.the-star.co.ke/health/2025-10-23-kenyan-creates-first-sign-language-app)); FingerTalk 1,000+ ([neohear](https://www.neohear.com/?p=6069)).
- Relay economics: TTRS about 40 interpreters for about 10k regular users ([Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbsworld.com/science-technology/the-impact-of-technology-on-the-hard-of-hearing)); DeafTawk 80/20 interpreter split ([GSMA 2023](https://www.gsma.com/get-involved/gsma-foundry/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/DeafTawk-Helping-The-Deaf-To-Communicate.pdf)); ISLRTC free via WhatsApp ([Annual Report](https://divyangjan.depwd.gov.in/upload/s3wassupload/ISLRTC_New_Delhi_Annual_Report_2023_24.pdf)).
- Funders active in these regions: GSMA Innovation Fund for Assistive Tech (FCDO-backed, $250k to DeafTawk), Prosus SICA (SignAble), USAID with Humanity & Inclusion (Mero Sanket; USAID's status after 2025 makes it unreliable), UNDP (BdSL dictionary), WISE / Qatar Foundation (Deaf Reach), the Royal Academy of Engineering Africa Prize (Signvrse), and telecom CSR (Dialog, Jazz, Digi, Turkcell, Umniah). — sources above.
- Open licences: OpenHands Apache-2.0 ([GitHub](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/OpenHands)); AUTSL academic-only ([Hamburg](https://www.sign-lang.uni-hamburg.de/lrec/data/autsl.html)); CASL-W60 academic-only ([Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/record/14734421)); INCLUDE unspecified ([Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/4010759)).

### Inferences
- Copy list for Uzbekistan:
  1. ISLRTC: dictionary, free relay over WhatsApp, and an online course.
  2. PanSALB: handshape search.
  3. DEP: Deaf crowd-contribution.
  4. DEAFinition: "request a word".
  5. Deaf Reach: school-network content production plus literacy stories.
  6. TTRS: multi-mode relay, but with diversified funding.
  7. Mada Jumla: embeddable widget for gov sites (later).
  8. ISH News: Deaf news channel.
- Use OpenHands (Apache-2.0) as a starting ISLR codebase, and publish the Uzbek data under CC BY 4.0 with explicit signer consent. That would make it one of very few openly licensed sign datasets in these regions.
- Telecom CSR (Ucell, Beeline UZ, Uzmobile) is the most repeated funding and distribution channel across Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Turkey and Jordan.

### Gaps
- No region-wide usage benchmarks (DAU, retention) exist publicly. Most app download counts were not retrievable in this pass.
