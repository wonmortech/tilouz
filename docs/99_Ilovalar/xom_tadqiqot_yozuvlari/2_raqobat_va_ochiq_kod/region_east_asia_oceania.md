# Deaf-communication technology in East Asia and Oceania (status Nov 2026)

Scope: South Korea, Japan, China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Mongolia, Australia, New Zealand. Apps, big-tech features, avatars/digital humans, state relay, open source, datasets, research.
Method note: 17 tool calls (search + fetch). Many official statistics pages (NIA Korea, Japanese MIC 5-year review, Access Hub NRS PDFs) were not fully readable in this session. Anything not verified is in "Gaps", not presented as fact. Items marked [BG] in Gaps come from the researcher's background knowledge and were NOT re-verified here.

## 1. South Korea: KSL avatars, 107 relay, datasets, policy

### Takeaway
Korea's sign-avatar market in 2025-2026 is driven by small social ventures (EQ4ALL, KL Cube) selling avatar-translation into government and enterprise touchpoints (disaster texts, kiosks, websites, banking), not by the big telcos. The state relay (107 Sonmal-ieum Center) is run by the government agency NIA since 2005; current volume figures were not found.

### Cited Findings
- EQ4ALL (이큐포올) is a B Corp-certified social venture developing AI-based avatar sign-language translation and other accessibility solutions — [seenthis.kr](https://seenthis.kr/newspageeng/4060?page=10); [kjob.news](https://www.kjob.news/news/431732)
- EQ4ALL exhibited at AI Festa 2025 (30 Sep - 2 Oct 2025, theme "Information for All") — [seenthis.kr](https://seenthis.kr/newspageeng/4060?page=10)
- EQ4ALL's AI avatar sign solution was reported as already applied to Jeju disaster text alerts, Ministry of Food and Drug Safety food QR service, National Pension Service and National Museum of Korea (shown at Korea Social Value Festa, Aug 2025, COEX) — [kjob.news](https://www.kjob.news/news/431732); [seenthis.kr](https://seenthis.kr/newspageeng/4898)
- EQ4ALL planned to show AI sign disaster-broadcasting solutions at World IT Show 2026 (Seoul, 22 Apr 2026) — [venturesquare](https://www.venturesquare.net/en/1125921/)
- KL Cube (케이엘큐브): "3D avatar AI sign translation service" shown at AI EXPO KOREA 2025; platform name HandSignVerse (핸드사인버스); in May 2026 applied on the COEX website main guide (click icon -> KSL video plays); linked to KakaoBank sign-language counselling and Seongdong-gu welfare-facility guidance — [Seoul Shinmun 2026-05-27](https://m.seoul.co.kr/news/economy/2026/05/27/20260527500189); [socialvalue.kr](https://www.socialvalue.kr/news/view/1065600755495883?dt=m)
- KT's verified sign-related offering is older: in 2020 KT and the Korea Deaf Association launched a video-call ("손말 영상통화") service with 2GB/day free dedicated data for KT customers registered as hearing-impaired — [Asia Economy 2020](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2020030908251195296)
- KT's 2026 AI store agent supports 20+ spoken languages, not sign language — [Korea Times 2026-06-15](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/companies/20260615/kt-rolls-out-ai-store-assistant-to-break-language-barriers-for-intl-customers)
- 107 Sonmal-ieum Center (손말이음센터): sign/text telecom relay operated by NIA (한국지능정보사회진흥원) since 2005; video relay via website and mobile phone — [futurechosun](https://futurechosun.com/archives/9642); [110.go.kr](https://www.110.go.kr/consult/cam.do)
- One article reports average daily usage of over 2,000 calls since the Nov 2005 launch (article year not clear, so not a current figure) — [futurechosun](https://futurechosun.com/archives/9642)
- 119 sign interpretation: MSIT and National Fire Agency opened a system where a Deaf caller, the 119 control room and a relay interpreter join a 3-way video call via the Sonmal-ieum Center — [korea.kr](https://m.korea.kr/news/policyNewsView.do?newsId=148941990)
- Labour issues: 2018 interview with the KT New Union branch head at the relay center said relay-interpreter working conditions were "very poor"; interpreters were to be directly hired by NIA; ~50% of all sign interpreters are in Seoul, so relocating centres makes hiring harder — [Weekly Khan 2018](https://weekly.khan.co.kr/article/201812241411571)
- Local sign-interpretation centres have chronic staff shortages, often only 4-5 interpreters per centre (2023) — [Segye 2023](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20230530514742)
- AI Hub KSL video data is widely used in Korean academic work; one paper reports BLEU-4 34.68 for translation using the AI Hub sign video dataset; papers describe it as large-scale, multi-signer, multi-angle — [Korea Univ. AML lab](https://aml.korea.ac.kr/research/sign-language-translation); [koreascience](https://www.koreascience.kr/article/CFKO202023758834200.pdf)
- A student project describes training on "56GB, millions of samples" of AI Hub sign data (unofficial figure) — [Sookmyung](https://software.sookmyung.ac.kr/bbs/software/722/100761/download.do)

### Inferences
- Korea's model = government procurement of avatar translation from small social ventures. For a non-profit, the reusable pattern is "template content" use-cases (disaster alerts, public service QR info, kiosks) where sentences are predictable, rather than open-domain translation.
- The 107 center shows the cost driver of relay is human interpreters and their working conditions, not technology.

### Gaps
- No current (2023-2026) annual 107 relay volume, budget or interpreter headcount found; the source is NIA annual statistics / National Assembly audit (국정감사) materials.
- AI Hub dataset official size and license were not confirmed. [BG] AI Hub data is generally free for Korean nationals/residents after application, research and commercial use allowed within Korea, with restrictions on overseas transfer; foreign access is limited. Must be checked on aihub.or.kr before relying on it.
- No 2025 KBS/MBC AI sign news avatar, Samsung, SK Telecom or Naver sign product was found in searches. [BG] KBS ran an AI sign-language weather/news avatar pilot (with EQ4ALL/ETRI-linked tech) around 2020-2022; SKT/Samsung had small CSR projects. Unverified, treat as possibly dead.
- Seoul subway / KTX sign displays: one dailian.co.kr snippet mentions a kiosk answering hearing-impaired passengers' questions with a sign avatar, vendor not identified.
- Korean Sign Language Act (2016) tech impact: no source retrieved in this session. [BG] The Act (한국수화언어법, effective Aug 2016) made KSL an official language equal to Korean and underpins public-sector demand for sign content and the National Institute of Korean Language's KSL dictionary/corpus.
- KAIST / ETRI research specifics not retrieved.

## 2. Japan: NHK CG, telephone relay law, apps, Shuwa project

### Takeaway
Japan has the world's clearest legislated public telephone relay service (since 2021) with ~14-19k registered users and ~30k calls/month (late 2023); NHK has the longest-running broadcaster sign-CG program (weather since 2017, AI translation demos 2024). The Google/CUHK/Kwansei Gakuin "Shuwa" toolkit is open source on GitHub; a separate 2026 academic J-Shuwa corpus (197k pairs, ~300h) is the largest public JSL dataset.

### Cited Findings
- Telephone relay service (電話リレーサービス), operated by the Nippon Foundation Telephone Relay Service: as of end Dec 2023 over 14,000 users, ~30,000 calls per month, over 210,000 minutes of interpreting per month — [NFTRS FY2024 paper](https://nftrs.or.jp/news/fy2024/files/NFTRSpapers_fy2024-001.pdf)
- A March 2026 industry document gives 19,000 users and 440,000 calls per year (period not specified) — [appie.or.jp 2026-03](https://appie.or.jp/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/20260324keisansyou1-2.pdf)
- MIC (総務省) published a five-year review of the relay service on 29 Oct 2025 — [soumu.go.jp PDF](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/001038069.pdf)
- Carriers (SoftBank, NTT, NTT West, Rakuten) publish annual notices around the relay levy (March 2025, March 2026) — [SoftBank press 2025-03-25](https://www.softbank.jp/corp/news/press/sbkk/2025/20250325_01/); [NTT West 2026-03-26](https://www.ntt-west.co.jp/newscms/info5/15132/oshirase20260326.pdf); [Rakuten Mobile](https://network.mobile.rakuten.co.jp/information/news/service/3314/)
- MIC runs relay-service seminars in 2025 (information accessibility portal) — [soumu.go.jp](https://www.soumu.go.jp/info-accessibility-portal/telecomrelay/seminar/2025/)
- NHK weather sign-CG site opened to the public on 20 Feb 2017; generates videos automatically from prefectural forecast telegrams, in principle 3 times a day — [NHK Foundation](https://www.nhk-fdn.or.jp/es/news/archive/shuwaCG.pdf); [MIC](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/000509122.pdf)
- 2022: NHK Osaka trial delivered weather (normal times) and disaster information (emergency) in sign CG — [MIC](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/000843755.pdf)
- NHK STRL Open House 2024: "translation AI" converting Japanese news text into sign-word sequences; motion generated with diffusion models, with facial-expression control — [Phileweb 2024-05](https://www.phileweb.com/news/d-av/202405/28/60433.html)
- NHK Foundation researches style-transfer AI converting live signer video into an anime-like style, so CG signing without motion-capture data; Japanese-to-JSL translation still needs free (non-literal) translation and remains a challenge; NHK Foundation also supports templates for weather and sports sign CG — [NHK Foundation lab](https://www.nhk-fdn.or.jp/es/assets/img/lab/pdf/004.pdf); [VIEW2021](https://www.nhk-fdn.or.jp/es/view/pdf/VIEW2021_40_6_11.pdf)
- Google "shuwa" GitHub repo: toolkit using JSL and HKSL video to train a baseline model for recognizing gestures and facial expressions; can be trained for any sign in any sign language — [github.com/google/shuwa](https://github.com/google/shuwa)
- Partners: Nippon Foundation + Chinese University of Hong Kong, with Google and Kwansei Gakuin University (KGU selected JSL words and collected JSL data); the 2021 output was SignTown, an online sign-learning game — [Nippon Foundation 2021](https://en.nippon-foundation.or.jp/news/articles/2021/20210524-58778.html); [TNF SignTown](https://tnfsa.nippon-foundation.or.jp/news/531449)
- Nippon Foundation later released a handbook app for learning Japanese, Hong Kong and Yangon sign languages — [TNF](https://tnfsa.nippon-foundation.or.jp/news/1736325); [Nippon Foundation 2023](https://en.nippon-foundation.or.jp/news/articles/2023/20230922-95355.html)
- J-Shuwa (ACL Findings 2026, Junwen Mo et al.): 197K parallel JSL-Japanese sentence pairs, ~300 hours, web-collected, "largest publicly available JSL dataset to date"; code/data at github.com/SpaJune/J-Shuwa — [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1821/). No link to the Google Shuwa project was found.

### Inferences
- Japan's relay is funded by a per-number levy collected through carriers (inferred from the carriers' annual notices) — the "universal service levy" model, the same family as Australia's.
- ~30k calls/month for ~14k users => ~2 calls per user per month; the service is used but small relative to Japan's Deaf population.
- NHK's success case is weather: structured input (forecast telegrams) -> template sign CG. Open-domain news translation is still at demo stage after 7+ years.
- Copy for an open platform: Google shuwa (training toolkit) + SignTown-style learning game; J-Shuwa's web-collected methodology is a template for building a corpus from TV/online signed content.

### Gaps
- Exact FY2024/FY2025 relay call counts, per-number levy amount and annual cost: in the MIC Oct 2025 review PDF, not extracted. [BG] The levy was 1 yen per number per year initially (2021 law: Act on Facilitation of Telephone Relay Service for Persons with Hearing Disabilities), changed in later years — verify.
- Google shuwa repo license not confirmed in this session ([BG] Google open-source repos are typically Apache-2.0). Whether the underlying JSL/HKSL video data was released is unconfirmed.
- J-Shuwa license not checked (web-collected video often restricts redistribution).
- SoftBank SureTalk, "Tsuyaku"/translation apps, Sony, Fujitsu, KDDI: not covered in retrieved sources. [BG] SoftBank's SureTalk (2016-ish, sign recognition + voice-to-text, with Fujitsu/SoftBank) is believed discontinued — verify.

## 3. China: sign digital humans, datasets, Deaf reception

### Takeaway
China deployed the most visible sign digital humans (Tencent "Lingyu" for CCTV Winter Olympics 2022, Alibaba DAMO "Xiaomo" for Hangzhou Asian Para Games, China Mobile/Migu World Cup avatar, Baidu Intelligent Cloud avatar), but the strongest independent evidence — by a Deaf professor — says Deaf viewers largely could not understand them. China also produced the largest open-for-research continuous sign datasets (CSL-Daily, CSL-News ~1,985h).

### Cited Findings
- Tencent Lingyu (聆语): 3D sign-language digital interpreter on CCTV for Beijing Winter Olympics 2022; used for China's first gold (mixed team short-track relay); Tencent claims vocabulary/sentence coverage >1.6 million and sign intelligibility >90% (vendor figures); launched on cctv.com; reads anchors' facial expressions/mouth movement to translate broadcast into signing; companion avatar Xiaocong for Tencent Sports — [Pandaily](https://pandaily.com/?p=91138); [Tencent](https://www.tencent.com/en-us/articles/2201380.html); [China Daily](https://regional.chinadaily.com.cn/wic/2024-05/23/c_989709_2.htm)
- Baidu Intelligent Cloud built "the first AI-driven sign-language digital human" of Baidu Intelligent Cloud, viral after a test video; no Olympic role confirmed — [besthub.dev](https://www.besthub.dev/articles/ai-sign-language-digital-human-technology-challenges-and-development-by-baidu-intelligent-cloud-0aa012514cf7)
- Alibaba DAMO Academy "Xiaomo" avatar for 2022 Hangzhou Asian Para Games; China Mobile + Migu Video avatar for 2022 World Cup sign commentary — [Sixth Tone 2025-07-09](https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1017316/The%20Promise%20and%20Pitfalls%20of%20China%E2%80%99s%20AI%20Sign%20Language%20Interpreters)
- Sixth Tone (9 Jul 2025, by Zheng Xuan, a Deaf university professor and former consultant on a major tech company's sign-avatar project): back-translation of Olympics avatar signing showed significant key information lost or distorted; Deaf viewers had trouble understanding (video and transcripts); handshapes, positions, movements, facial expressions, mouth movements differed from everyday CSL; avatars coped with sentences sharing Chinese word order but failed on spatial/simultaneous constructions ("fish swimming in water"); Deaf viewers and interpreters largely sceptical, limited vocabulary, problems with polysemous words; avatars best received by non-Deaf audiences; developers kept Deaf people and interpreters in supporting roles — [Sixth Tone](https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1017316/The%20Promise%20and%20Pitfalls%20of%20China%E2%80%99s%20AI%20Sign%20Language%20Interpreters)
- Same article: 20.5 million people in China have hearing disabilities (national survey); cites Wu Ling finding no Chinese equivalents for 50% of CSL gestures; interpreter shortage, few universities offer interpreting majors — [Sixth Tone](https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1017316/The%20Promise%20and%20Pitfalls%20of%20China%E2%80%99s%20AI%20Sign%20Language%20Interpreters)
- XJTLU (2025) runs a sign-language AI project — [XJTLU](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/news/2025/06/breaking-barriers-with-a-sign-language-ai-project)
- CSL-Daily (USTC, CVPR 2021): daily-life Chinese sign language corpus; released to universities and research institutes for research only via signed release agreement; contacts Prof. Wengang Zhou and Houqiang Li — [USTC CSL-Daily page](https://ustc-slr.github.io/datasets/2021_csl_daily); [arXiv 2105.12397](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.12397v1); [TIB](https://service.tib.eu/ldmservice/dataset/csl-daily)
- CSL-News (Uni-Sign paper, 2025): 1,985 hours of CSL video with text annotations — [arXiv 2501.15187](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.15187v2)

### Inferences
- China's lesson is negative and important: vendor "90% intelligibility" claims vs. Deaf-led evaluation showing poor comprehension. An open platform should make Deaf-led evaluation (back-translation by Deaf signers) a built-in, public metric.
- Big-event showcase avatars (Olympics, Para Games, World Cup) were PR deployments; no evidence of sustained daily use was found.
- Datasets: research-only release agreements (CSL-Daily) block use in an open-source product's released model weights unless licensing is negotiated; CSL-News-style web/TV-news harvesting (1,985h) is the scalable path.

### Gaps
- Huawei StorySign: no 2023-2026 source retrieved. [BG] Launched 2018 with the European Union of the Deaf (children's book reading with avatar "Star", multiple European sign languages); likely inactive/dead — verify.
- iFlytek sign products, national CSL standard (国家通用手语, 2018) and dictionaries, Chinese Deaf apps: not retrieved. [BG] The "National Common Sign Language Dictionary" (国家通用手语词典) was published 2019 after the 2018 standard; China Disabled Persons' Federation runs an online version/app — verify.
- Tencent Lingyu post-2022 status (still on CCTV? open API?) not confirmed.

## 5. Australia: NRS, Auslan

### Takeaway
Australia's National Relay Service is a government-contracted service (provider: Concentrix) funded by the Telecommunications Industry Levy on carriers, with public monthly performance reports; the 2026-27 Budget added further funding.

### Cited Findings
- NRS funded via the Telecommunications Industry Levy (Telecommunications (Industry Levy) Act 2012) on eligible carriers; 2025-26 eligible revenue determination — [legislation.gov.au F2025L01137](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L01137/asmade/2025-09-23/es/original/pdf); [F2025L00181](https://legislation.gov.au/F2025L00181/asmade)
- Provider Concentrix reports monthly to the Department of Infrastructure; July 2025 report: 14,287 emergency calls in one table, 93.88% of calls answered within 30 seconds (partial snippet data) — [NRS July 2025 report](https://www.accesshub.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/2025-07-monthly-performance-report-july.pdf)
- April 2026 report: outbound connections e.g. 2,627 for NRS SMS and 1,778 for NRS Chat (partial) — [NRS April 2026](https://www.accesshub.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/national-relay-service-monthly-performance-report-april-2026.pdf)
- Deaf Australia: 2026-27 Federal Budget confirmed additional NRS funding; implementation details not yet released — [Deaf Australia](https://www.deafaustralia.org.au/news/federal-budget-2026-27---advocacy-win)
- Sign Bridge AI (third party) offers live sign translation in ASL, BSL and Auslan, launched 2 Nov 2025 — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/sign-bridge-ai-2/launches)

### Inferences
- Australia publishes monthly KPIs (answer within 30s, emergency calls by channel) — a transparency model worth copying for any Uzbek relay pilot.

## 7. Cross-region lessons for an open-source, non-profit platform

### Takeaway
Across the region, what works in production is (a) human-interpreter relay funded by a telecom levy with public KPIs (Japan, Australia, Korea), and (b) avatars on structured/templated content (weather, transport, disaster alerts); open-domain AI avatars remain poorly understood by Deaf viewers.

### Cited Findings
- Templated success cases: NHK weather CG from forecast telegrams — [NHK Foundation](https://www.nhk-fdn.or.jp/es/news/archive/shuwaCG.pdf); EQ4ALL disaster alerts/QR — [kjob.news](https://www.kjob.news/news/431732); Kara transport alerts — [AT](https://at.govt.nz/nzsl-app-alerts)
- Open-domain failure evidence: Deaf-led evaluation of China's Olympics avatars — [Sixth Tone](https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1017316/The%20Promise%20and%20Pitfalls%20of%20China%E2%80%99s%20AI%20Sign%20Language%20Interpreters)
- Openly reusable assets: google/shuwa toolkit — [GitHub](https://github.com/google/shuwa); J-Shuwa corpus — [ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1821/); CSL-Daily (research-only) — [USTC](https://ustc-slr.github.io/datasets/2021_csl_daily)

### Inferences
- Copy: levy-funded relay + monthly public KPIs; templated avatar pipelines; Deaf-led naming/co-design (Tūī); Deaf back-translation as quality metric; web-harvested parallel corpus methodology (J-Shuwa, CSL-News).
- Avoid: showcase avatars for big events without Deaf evaluation; research-only data licenses that cannot ship in open weights.

### Gaps
- No region-wide cost-per-minute comparison of relay services could be compiled from retrieved sources.
