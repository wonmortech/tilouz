# Uzbek Sign Language (UzSL / O'zbek imo-ishora tili): linguistics, Deaf education, online community (as of Nov 2026)

Research note: about 22 tool calls. Many primary pages (web.archive.org, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) were not fetched or could not be fetched. Items with no source are in the Gaps sections only.

## 1. Linguistics: UzSL vs RSL, regional variants, fingerspelling, descriptions, catalog entries

### Takeaway
Uzbek Deaf researchers and the English-language reference sources describe UzSL as a variety/dialect of Russian Sign Language (RSL) with local Uzbek-specific signs. I found no published lexicon-share estimate. Fingerspelling is in transition: the Cyrillic (Russian) dactyl alphabet is still widely used, and a Latin dactyl alphabet is taught unevenly across schools. Regional and generational variation in dactyl alphabets is documented. Dictionary resources go back to the 2005 Society of the Deaf/JICA dictionary. A new online dictionary (imo-ishora.uz, 2000+ signs) was built by the NGO Sharoit Plyus over 2025-2026.

### Cited Findings
- Wikipedia's RSL article lists "Uzbek Sign Language" (with Kazakh SL) as a dialect of RSL. RSL codes: ISO 639-3 `rsl`, Glottolog `russ1270`. — [Wikipedia: Russian Sign Language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Sign_Language)
- "Uzbek Sign Language is a dialect of RSL, and while it has many phrases different from RSL, it is not recognized by the government as an official language." — [Wikipedia: Deafness in Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deafness_in_Uzbekistan), citing [Yusupov 2021, CREID Working Paper 8](https://opendocs.ids.ac.uk/opendocs/bitstream/handle/20.500.12413/16710/CREID_Working_Paper_8.pdf)
- The "to be or not to be" debate (Dilmurad Yusupov, Gazeta.uz, 23 Sep 2020): Uzbekistan uses RSL. Cited studies find the sign languages of Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan "differ little" from RSL, with differences from local cultural context. In the author's view it has "become a dialect of RSL", and whether it can be called "Uzbek Sign Language" is an open question. — [Gazeta.uz 2020, "Быть ли узбекскому жестовому языку?"](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2020/09/23/sign-language/)
- Gazeta.uz 2022 (Ahliddinov and Yusupov): O'IT is "a variety (shevasi) of RIT" with Uzbek-specific signs added. A "street sign language" (ko'cha imo-ishora tili) is used among less-educated deaf people. — [Gazeta.uz 2022, "Biz haqimizda bizsiz qaror"](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2022/04/22/sign-language/)
- Regional/generational variation: deaf schools in Jizzakh, Surxondaryo, Bukhara, Samarkand and Olmaliq use **different dactyl alphabets**, and communication between older and younger signers can break down. — [Gazeta.uz 2022](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2022/04/22/sign-language/)
- Latin dactyl: XTV (Ministry of Public Education) worked with representatives on a Latin dactyl alphabet in late 2020. A Cabinet of Ministers draft resolution "Imo-ishora tilini rivojlantirish to'g'risida" (first draft 2 Dec 2020, revised draft on regulation.gov.uz ID 56302) gave XTV and the Finance Ministry three months to produce a Latin-based Uzbek dactyl alphabet. At the time of the article the draft was not adopted. The Surxondaryo deaf boarding school was the only one "still fully learning" the Latin dactyl. Cyrillic dactyl is still widely used. The letters "L" and "D" differ substantially between the two systems. — [Gazeta.uz 2022](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2022/04/22/sign-language/)
- Uncertainty around the Latin alphabet itself (from March 2021, debate on whether Sh, Ch, G', O' might change) made the dactyl's final form uncertain. — [Gazeta.uz 2022](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2022/04/22/sign-language/)
- An academic recognition-algorithm paper says Uzbekistan currently uses both Russian (Cyrillic) and Latin manual alphabets, and builds a dataset on the Uzbek Latin manual alphabet. — [CyberLeninka: "O'zbek ishora tili harflarini tanib olish algoritmi"](https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/o-zbek-ishora-tili-harflarini-tanib-olish-algoritmi/pdf)
- PQ-407 (20.10.2022, "O'zbek imo-ishora tili va Brayl alifbosini rivojlantirish bo'yicha qo'shimcha chora-tadbirlar to'g'risida") is the main legal act. The norma.uz summary of it does not mention a Latin dactyl. — [norma.uz](https://www.norma.uz/oz/qonunchilikda_yangi/uzbek_imo-ishora_tili_va_brayl_alifbosini_rivojlantirish_buyicha_chora-tadbirlar_belgilandi)
- PQ-407 set a **1 June 2024 deadline for a UzSL linguistic dictionary and teaching materials**. Per Gazeta.uz (Sept 2025), public information shows the deadline was missed, and the online dictionary is being developed by the NGO Sharoit Plyus. — [Gazeta.uz, 28 Sep 2025 (ru)](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/09/28/sign-language/); [Gazeta.uz, 30 Sep 2025 (oz)](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2025/09/30/sign-language/)
- 2005 dictionary: the first O'IT dictionary, "O'zbekiston karlarining imo-ishorali nutqi", was published in 2005 by the Uzbekistan Society of the Deaf with JICA support, in a limited print run. According to the article, no systematic dictionary work followed for 17+ years. — [Gazeta.uz 2022](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2022/04/22/sign-language/)
- Sharoit Plyus project "Developing Uzbek sign language online dictionary website and mobile app". Donor: The Stirling Foundation (USA). Budget USD 30,120. Dates 14 May 2025 to 14 May 2026. National partner: National Agency of Social Protection. Stated rationale: lack of standardized resources, earlier dictionary from 2005. Target groups include about 5,000 students and staff of deaf boarding schools and parents of deaf children. — [Sharoit Plus projects](https://sharoitplus.uz/category/loyihalar/)
- imo-ishora.uz (UzSL dictionary): promotes "2000+ so'z" with video demonstrations. Categories shown: Uzbek **Latin** alphabet (dactyl), numbers, seasons, days, months, colors, greetings, family. Users can search and suggest new signs (with image upload). Partners listed: Deaf Reach, Family Educational Services Foundation (FESF, programming), Sharoit Plus, The Stirling Foundation. "Install app" link (PWA-like, platform not stated). No regional-variant tagging is visible. — [imo-ishora.uz](https://imo-ishora.uz/)
- Academic/teaching descriptions exist in Uzbek: Nizomiy TDPU lecture complexes by Z.N. Mamarajabova on "Daktilologiya va imo-ishora nutqi" and "O'zbek imo-ishora nutqi". — [devedu.uz PDF 3](https://devedu.uz/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/3-Daktilologiya-va-imo-ishora-nutqi.pdf); [devedu.uz PDF 2](https://devedu.uz/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/2-Ozbek-imo-ishora-nutqi-yangi-majmua.pdf); [devedu.uz PDF 4](https://devedu.uz/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/4-Daktil-va-imo-ishora-nutqiga-orgatish.pdf)
- Small linguistic papers (conference/journal level) on UzSL phonology and semantics, e.g. on distinguishing meaning by changing substance, direction and quality of movement with the same handshape, and on ways of expressing sign meanings. — [econfseries.com article 788](https://econfseries.com/index.php/4/article/view/788); [phoenixpublication.net 4499](https://phoenixpublication.net/index.php/TANQ/article/view/4499)

### Inferences
- For data collection, treat UzSL as RSL-based with local lexicon. Annotation schemas should tag **fingerspelling system (Cyrillic vs Latin)**, **signer age cohort**, and **school/region of schooling**, because dactyl alphabets differ by school and generation. The Gazeta 2022 evidence points to schooling origin as more predictive than geography alone.
- The imo-ishora.uz dictionary (2000+ signs, Latin dactyl) is the de facto current reference and a natural partner/benchmark. Its team (Sharoit Plyus) already combines Deaf-community experts and interpreters.
- Mouthing is likely mixed Russian/Uzbek, depending on the signer's schooling language. This is not confirmed by any source found (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No quantitative lexicon-share estimate (UzSL vs RSL) found in any source.
- No documentation of Tashkent / Samarkand / Fergana / Khorezm / Karakalpakstan variants specifically. Only school-level dactyl differences (Jizzakh, Surxondaryo, Bukhara, Samarkand, Olmaliq) are documented.
- Mouthing (Uzbek vs Russian): no source found.
- Kun.uz article on the Latin dactyl: not found by search. No evidence found of a formally approved (gazetted) Latin dactyl alphabet. The 2020-2022 Cabinet draft appears unadopted as of 2022, and PQ-407's summary does not mention it.
- Glottolog/Ethnologue: no separate UzSL entry found. It appears subsumed under RSL (`rsl` / `russ1270`), but this was not verified directly on glottolog.org.
- The 2005 dictionary on unilibrary.uz and dissertations at TDPU: not located or verified.
- The Yusupov 2020 original blog post (dilmurad.me) was unreachable (archive.org blocked). The Gazeta 2020 version was used instead.

## 2. Education: schools for deaf/HoH children, language of instruction, reforms, university programs

### Takeaway
About 19 boarding schools (plus 15 preschool institutions/groups) serve deaf and hard-of-hearing children, with about 5,000 pupils (2019 figure). Instruction has historically been oralist (lip-reading, articulation), and most teachers lacked sign proficiency. PQ-407 (2022) requires that, from 2024/2025, special schools hire only teachers who know the sign language used in instruction. A bachelor's program in sign-language interpreting (Maxsus pedagogika: surdotarjimonlik faoliyati) started in 2023, with first graduates expected in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- "19 maktab internati and 15 maktabgacha tarbiya muassasa va guruhlar" for deaf and HoH children (journal article, ~2025). — [ResearchGate: "Eshitishida nuqsoni bo'lgan bolalar ta'limining rivojlanish tarixi"](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391882672_ESHITISHIDA_NUQSONI_BO'LGAN_BOLALAR_TA'LIMINING_RIVOJLANISH_TARIXI) (per search snippet; full text not fetched)
- About 5,000 deaf and HoH students attended specialized deaf schools in 2019. — [Yusupov 2021, CREID WP8](https://opendocs.ids.ac.uk/opendocs/bitstream/handle/20.500.12413/16710/CREID_Working_Paper_8.pdf) via [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deafness_in_Uzbekistan). Sharoit Plus also cites "about 5,000 students and staff of deaf boarding schools". — [Sharoit Plus](https://sharoitplus.uz/category/loyihalar/)
- Many teachers at specialized schools do not know sign language. Schools emphasize lip-reading and articulation (oralism), and pupils' signing is often restricted. Pupils finish a year later than mainstream peers because of the longer curriculum. A 2017 presidential decree ordered sign-language courses in regional centres, which were "never organized". — [Gazeta.uz 2020](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2020/09/23/sign-language/)
- Example school: specialized boarding school No. 102 for hard-of-hearing children, Olmazor district, Tashkent (presidential visit, April 2022). At that visit, the Ministry was tasked with Zamin Foundation to examine 2,600+ hard-of-hearing children and provide hearing aids. — [president.uz](https://president.uz/oz/lists/view/5117) (per search snippet)
- Class-size norms: for partly-hearing children, 10 (primary) / 12 (upper grades). For children with multiple impairments, 6 / 8. — [gendermadad.uz](https://gendermadad.uz/oz/document/1740) (per search snippet)
- PQ-407 (20.10.2022), with measures from 1 Jan 2023: UzSL and Braille courses in general schools and "Ishga marhamat" monocentres (free for relatives of minors and for people with disabilities). Admission quotas for state-grant Surdopedagogika/Tiflopedagogika places +25% for 2023/2024. Compulsory sign-language/Braille exams for graduates of those programs. Sign interpreters provided at university entrance exams. From 2024/2025, deaf schools hire only teachers who know the instruction sign language. Annual "Best sign language interpreter" contest each September from 2023 (prizes 100/65/50 BHM). At least 5% of weekly airtime on state TV with sign interpretation, closed subtitles or scrolling text. — [norma.uz on PQ-407](https://www.norma.uz/oz/qonunchilikda_yangi/uzbek_imo-ishora_tili_va_brayl_alifbosini_rivojlantirish_buyicha_chora-tadbirlar_belgilandi)
- University program: "Maxsus pedagogika: surdotarjimonlik faoliyati" appears in the 2025/2026 bachelor's lists. Codes conflict across sources (60110300 vs 60110305). Entrance subjects: Kasbiy (ijodiy imtihon) + Ona tili va adabiyoti. — [oliygoh.uz fanlar majmuasi](https://oliygoh.uz/post/20252026-yil-bakalavriat-yonalishlari-boyicha-fanlar-majmuasi-tasdiqlandi); [oliygoh.uz TDPU](https://oliygoh.uz/oliygohlar/toshkent-davlat-pedagogika-universiteti)
- Since 2023 the National Pedagogical University (formerly Nizomiy TDPU) and several other universities offer a bachelor's for sign-language interpreters, with the **first graduates expected in 2026**. — [Gazeta.uz Sept 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/09/28/sign-language/)
- Before formal training existed, interpreters were mostly CODAs (children of deaf adults). — [Gazeta.uz Sept 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/09/28/sign-language/); [Gazeta.uz 2020](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2020/09/23/sign-language/)
- First international-sign course in Uzbekistan: International Inclusive Hub (under the National Agency of Social Protection), Jan-Jun 2025, 72 academic hours. 52 applications, 15 selected (7 professional interpreters + 8 people with hearing impairments). — [Gazeta.uz Jul 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/07/15/sign-language/)
- Admissions quota of 2% for deaf/HoH students is mentioned. There are reports of deaf students in mainstream university groups without interpreting (e.g. 4 deaf students in one National University sports group). — [Gazeta.uz 2022](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2022/04/22/sign-language/)

### Inferences
- The pupil base is about 5,000 children in about 19 schools. These schools are the most concentrated, reachable population for content testing and data collection, but they need Ministry of Preschool and School Education permission and parental consent (minors).
- The 2026 first cohort of interpreter graduates is a timely recruiting pool for annotators/validators. Their fingerspelling is likely Latin-first, while older Deaf adults are often Cyrillic-first.

### Gaps
- No full list of the 18/19 schools with locations and per-school enrollment was found. Statistics Agency (siat.stat.uz) tables exist but were not opened.
- No figures found for current teacher sign proficiency or for compliance with the 2024/2025 teacher-hiring rule.
- No enrollment numbers found for TDPU/Nizomiy surdopedagogika or surdotarjimonlik.
- Tashkent Medical Academy program: no source found.
- No sources found on inclusive-education reforms 2020-2026 beyond PQ-407 and the 2021 placement regulation.

## 3. Online community: channels, activity, topics, needs

### Takeaway
The Deaf online presence I could verify is small and NGO-centred. The official Society of the Deaf blog channel on Telegram (@uzsduz) had about 854-981 subscribers. The Sharoit Plyus network (Dilmurod Yusupov, Mamur Axlidinov) runs dictionary, legal-aid and video-interpreting projects. The recurring community demands are interpreter access (about 10 qualified interpreters for about 5,000 deaf people in Tashkent), legal recognition, TV interpretation and subtitles, and employment.

### Cited Findings
- @uzsduz, "O'zbekiston karlar jamiyati blogi / Блог общества глухих Узбекистана". 854 subscribers at fetch time (981 at index time), 849 videos. Bilingual Uzbek Latin + Russian, with sign-language videos (e.g. the national anthem). Topics: community events/exhibitions, International Deaf Week (21-27 Sept), statistics, online lectures (Dilmurod Yusupov on inclusive communication), and a research call about cochlear-implant users who stopped using their implant. No visible job posts. — [t.me/s/uzsduz](https://t.me/s/uzsduz); [telemetr.io](https://telemetr.io/en/channels/1481074705-uzsduz/posts)
- @imo_ishora_uz: official channel of the imo-ishora.uz dictionary. 11 subscribers, essentially empty. — [t.me/s/imo_ishora_uz](https://t.me/s/imo_ishora_uz)
- @deaf_gluxoyy "DEAF....ГЛУХОЙ": about 2.1K subscribers, news category (TGStat, UZ segment). Official status unknown. — [TGStat](https://uz.tgstat.com/en/channel/@deaf_gluxoyy)
- TV: "Uzbekistan 24" airs sign-interpreted news twice daily (14:00 and 15:00, 15-20 min). "Toshkent", "Mahalla", "Yoshlar" and "Sport" add interpretation only occasionally. No standard subtitles on national TV or YouTube. The interpreter window does not meet the international standard (25-30% of screen). — [Gazeta.uz Sept 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/09/28/sign-language/)
- Needs survey ("Yuksalish" movement, 2024, n=102): 56% "always/often" face difficulties using sign language. Nearly 90% face such problems periodically or constantly. 6% never do. — [Gazeta.uz Sept 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/09/28/sign-language/)
- Community demands, as stated by the authors of the Gazeta.uz Sept 2025 article: legal recognition of UzSL (amend Art. 28 of the disability law, which treats sign language only as a "means of interpersonal communication"); guaranteed annual interpreting hours (Russia guarantees 40 h/yr, Kazakhstan 30 h/yr); a central interpreter database with online booking; a national interpreter standard and certification; deaf interpreters; mandatory TV interpretation and subtitles; working surdo-online tablets in public service centres; completion of the dictionary. — [Gazeta.uz Sept 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/09/28/sign-language/)
- Interpreter scarcity: about 10 qualified interpreters in Tashkent for about 8,000 people with hearing disabilities in Tashkent region (about 1 per 800). — [Gazeta.uz Jul 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/07/15/sign-language/); [Gazeta.uz 2020](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2020/09/23/sign-language/)
- The deaf population: 21,212 registered with the Society of the Deaf (2019). Over 22,000 members and about 8,000 children with hearing disabilities (2025). — [Gazeta.uz 2020](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2020/09/23/sign-language/); [Gazeta.uz Sept 2025](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/09/28/sign-language/)
- Jobs: Soviet-era Society enterprises and "gorodok glukhikh" collapsed, leaving about 10 training facilities serving about 240-250 people. Deaf men often do informal roadside trade in Tashkent. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deafness_in_Uzbekistan), citing [Gazeta.uz 2019](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2019/04/17/subtitles/) and CREID WP8. Recent employer initiative: Yandex Lavka hires hearing-impaired warehouse staff, and Sharoit Plyus provides interpreters and communication training. — [Gazeta.uz Jun 2026](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2026/06/03/yandex-lavka-disabled/)
- Sharoit Plyus HuquqPlus projects (UNDP/Finland via the Ministry of Justice): a video-call centre with sign-language interpreting (2025, USD 9,000; and 1 Aug-31 Dec 2026, 238.25M UZS), plus 12 legal articles + 50 video reels in UzSL (2026). About 72+ legal articles previously produced in sign language, easy-read and audio formats. — [Sharoit Plus projects](https://sharoitplus.uz/category/loyihalar/)
- Deaf sport: at the Tokyo 2025 Deaflympics, Uzbek taekwondo athletes won 10 medals (1 gold, 4 silver, 5 bronze) per UzA. The team also entered football, judo, freestyle/Greco-Roman wrestling and table tennis. Wikipedia's Deaflympics table shows 0 for 2025 and appears outdated. — [UzA](https://uza.uz/en/posts/uzbekistans-taekwondo-athletes-completed-the-tokyo-2025-deaflympics-with-10-medals_788172); [UzA pre-Games](https://uza.uz/react/posts/uzbekistans-athletes-to-attend-tokyo-2025-deaflympics_783467); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbekistan_at_the_Deaflympics). An anhor.uz headline credits Guzal Eshmirzayeva with Uzbekistan's first-ever Deaflympic gold (event not verified). — [anhor.uz](https://anhor.uz/news/eshmirzayeva/)
- The Cultural Centre for Deaf People (Tashkent) received a 2014 award in connection with JICA, and JICA supported the 2005 dictionary. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deafness_in_Uzbekistan); [Gazeta.uz 2022](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2022/04/22/sign-language/)

### Inferences
- The Telegram audiences are small (hundreds to about 2K). Early-user acquisition will run through organizations (Society of the Deaf blog, Sharoit Plyus, the deaf sports federation, schools) rather than mass channels.
- The strongest product hooks are interpreter access (booking/video relay), subtitles for news, and legal/government information in UzSL. These are the documented, repeated demands.
- Content must be bilingual Uzbek Latin + Russian. The official Deaf channel already publishes in parallel.

### Gaps
- Instagram, TikTok and YouTube deaf-led accounts were not identified (not searched or fetched within budget). Deaf vloggers are unverified.
- "Axborot 24 surdo" was not specifically verified. Only Uzbekistan 24's sign-interpreted news slots (14:00/15:00) are confirmed.
- Deaf theatre in Tashkent: no source found. "Teatr mimiki i zhesta" search results refer to the Moscow theatre only.
- No subscriber counts or activity data for Sharoit Plyus's own channels.

## 5. Hearing parents of deaf children: communities, cochlear implants, foundations

### Takeaway
Zamin Foundation is the main documented actor in hearing screening and cochlear implants (thousands screened, about 23-45 operations per year in 2020-2022 reports). The state provides free cochlear implants only for children up to age 5. I found no organized parent-community channels with measurable size.

### Cited Findings
- Zamin Foundation: one project screened 4,626 people with hearing loss and performed 122 cochlear and bone-conduction implant operations, at the Vohidov Republican Surgical Centre, the National Children's Medical Centre, the Tashkent Pediatric Medical Institute and 5 regional centres. — [uz.wikipedia: Zamin fondi](https://uz.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamin_fondi)
- 2020: 23 children (Sep-Nov 2020) had their hearing restored (Gazeta). 36 cochlear/bone implant surgeries in 2020 (Kun.uz). — [Gazeta.uz 2020](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2020/12/19/zamin/); [Kun.uz 2020](https://kun.uz/news/2020/12/18/zamin-fondi-dasturi-doirasida-jami-23ta-jarrohlik-amaliyoti-otkazilib-bolalarning-eshitish-qobiliyati-tiklandi)
- 2022: 45 planned operations carried out, and about 300 hearing aids distributed under "Men eshitishni xohlayman!". — [Sputnik O'zbekiston](https://oz.sputniknews.uz/20221202/men-eshitishni-xohlayman---loyihasi-doirasida-300ga-yaqin-eshitish-apparati-tarqatildi-30455331.html)
- State program: free cochlear implants for children up to 5 with intact intellect. Older children pay themselves (about 130M soum, 2018). There are queues: one Khorezm family had waited 2 years. — [Kun.uz 2018](https://kun.uz/news/2018/10/06/uzbekistonda-kohlear-implantat-muammosiga-ecim-topadimi-2-jillik-navbatda-turgan-horazmlik-ota-ona-tasvisi); selection regulation No. 2648 (08.01.2015) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-2534269)

- PQ-407 gives relatives of minors with disabilities free UzSL courses (from 2023). — [norma.uz](https://www.norma.uz/oz/qonunchilikda_yangi/uzbek_imo-ishora_tili_va_brayl_alifbosini_rivojlantirish_buyicha_chora-tadbirlar_belgilandi)
- A Deaf-community research call (on @uzsduz) targets people with cochlear implants who don't use them. This points to implant non-use and the need for rehabilitation as a community topic. — [t.me/s/uzsduz](https://t.me/s/uzsduz)

### Inferences
- About 8,000 children with hearing disabilities (Gazeta 2025) and predominantly hearing families point to a parent audience that needs beginner UzSL content (family, greetings, numbers) and implant/rehab guidance. The free PQ-407 courses suggest an institutional channel (schools, monocentres).
