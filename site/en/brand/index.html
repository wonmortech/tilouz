Brand guidelines

Two halves of one conversation.

Tilo connects Deaf, hard-of-hearing and non-speaking people with hearing people. The logo says the same thing: two halves that meet, and a dot that turns speech into text.

One system, two roles

The founder chose two directions, 01 Geometrik and 05 Monogram. They are drawn from one geometry and used together, each where it is strongest.

01 Geometrik

The symbol

Two half-rings form a wave, a gold dot sits above it. In motion the halves join into an “o” ring: connected.

App icon, avatar, favicon, loaders, call states, 3D.

05 Monogram

The wordmark

The t-stem drops into the wave’s right half and rises as the i-stem; the dot is the i-dot. The wave is written as the name.

The name everywhere: headers, documents, the main logo.

The main logo

Why 05 never stands alone

Alone, the original 05 mark (wave + i) reads as “ni” or “ui”, and next to the word the i appeared twice. Now the monogram is the word’s own i, so the logo reads “tilo” once and only once.

Shared geometry

  • 1.3×wave radius to stroke: the counter is 1.6× stroke, close to phi
  • 1.07×dot diameter to stroke: a free circle looks smaller than a bar
  • 0.5×dot gap to stroke, in the symbol and in the wordmark
  • 1gold dot per composition: next to the symbol the i-dot takes the ink colour

Lockups and colourways

Four arrangements, five colourways. Pick the colourway for the background, never the other way round.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep two dot diameters free on every side. Use the dot of the logo you are placing.

Do and don’t

  • Use the files as they are Pick a colourway, keep the proportions.
  • Give it room Two dot diameters of clear space.
  • Choose the right background Light colourway on light, dark on dark.

Photography and video

  • Real Deaf people, real interpreters, real places.
  • Hands and face always in frame: the face is grammar.
  • Written consent in the person’s language, explained in sign language.
  • Captions always on. Sound is never the only channel.

Voice and tone

Tilo talks like a good interpreter: clear, warm, calm, and never in the way.

  • Clear, everyday words
  • Warm and equal
  • Calm, even in emergencies
  • Honest about mistakes
  • Puns that do not translate into sign
  • Pity: “suffers from deafness”
  • Shouting, ALL CAPS
  • “100% accurate”

Accessibility

Tilo exists for accessibility, so the brand meets it first: WCAG 2.2 AA everywhere, AAA for captions.

  • Contrast

    Text 4.5:1, large text and UI 3:1. Gold is never text.

  • Captions

    On every video, in Uzbek and Russian, with an interpreter for speech.

  • Reduced motion

    Every animation stops; state is still shown in words.

  • Not by colour alone

    Status is colour, icon and word together.

  • Targets

    At least 44 by 44 px, visible focus ring.

  • Plain language

    Short sentences; easy to read and easy to sign.
