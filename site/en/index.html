01Tilo · non-profit · open source

The doctor is talking. She cannot hear.

What if words were visible?

Let's take the wall down together.

A non-profit, open-source platform that removes the communication barrier between Deaf, hard-of-hearing and non-speaking people and hearing people. Uzbekistan first, then the CIS. This page is for volunteer developers, designers, ML people, Deaf community members and interpreters.

Tasavvur qiling: poliklinika. Shifokor gapiryapti, qarshisida kar ayol. Tovush to‘lqinlari bor, lekin ular devorga urilib qoladi. Bu devor — eshitish emas, muloqot tizimining yo‘qligi.

Agar so‘z ko‘rinadigan bo‘lsa-chi? Devor qulaydi, nutq subtitrga aylanadi. Bu bizning birinchi modulimiz — yuzma-yuz subtitr.

Ikki yarim — kar odam va eshituvchi odam — uchrashadi. Bu Tilo belgisi: ikki qism, bitta to‘lqin, ustida oltin nuqta. Tilo — notijorat, ochiq kodli platforma va biz unga ko‘ngillilarni chaqiramiz.

06a T9WFD/WASLI 2018

A 98% model fails on sentences

A model that recognises isolated signs at 98% does not work on continuous signing: signs run together. AI may only suggest a top 3; the Deaf user confirms.

Data Fest 202506a M16

Ko‘pchilik so‘raydi: ChatGPT’ga videoni bersak bo‘lmaydimi? Ochiq benchmark FLEURS-ASL’da GPT-4o 0.1 BLEU oldi, Gemini 0.2, Claude 3 Opus 0.4.

10What Tilo is

A free, open-source platform run by the Deaf community

Vision: Deaf, hard-of-hearing and non-speaking people in Uzbekistan, then the CIS, understand and are understood in their own language, anywhere, any time. 06a §1

  1. A shorter path to a live interpreter

    One tap, no login, honest hours and wait time.

  2. Face-to-face talk

    Live Uzbek/Russian captions, ready phrases, situation cards.

  3. The first open dataset

    Uzbek Sign Language: Deaf signers, layered consent, paid work.

Tiloning missiyasi: kar jamoasi boshqaradigan, bepul, ochiq kodli platforma.

U uchta ishni qiladi: jonli tarjimonga yo‘lni qisqartiradi, yuzma-yuz suhbatni osonlashtiradi va o‘zbek imo-ishora tili uchun birinchi ochiq, rozilikka asoslangan datasetni yaratadi.

11Principles

"Nothing about us without us": nine rules

Deaf-led

Deaf majority on the board; veto over every AI feature.

Free

Deaf users never pay. No session limits.

Open

Code Apache-2.0, data CC BY-SA / CC BY.

Privacy

Calls are not recorded; face video stays in Uzbekistan.

Honest hours

Hours and wait shown openly. "24/7" only when staffed.

Video first

Video, then plain text, with icons.

Reliability first

Call alerts and video stability measured every release.

Integrate

No new social network: entry via Telegram and WhatsApp.

No AI where it is risky

Medicine, court, police, emergencies — humans only.

06a §3 · T1–T9

To‘qqizta tamoyil. Eng muhimi birinchisi: «Biz haqimizda — bizsiz hech narsa». Kar jamoasi kengashda ko‘pchilik va har AI funksiyasiga veto huquqiga ega.

Qolganlari amaliy: bepul, ochiq, maxfiy, halol soatlar. Masalan, «24/7» deb faqat haqiqatan odam navbatchi bo‘lsa yozamiz.

13M1 · Interpreter (VRI)

No login, two taps — and you are in the queue, with your position and wait shown openly

  • An unregistered user reaches the queue in 2 taps
  • Position and wait refresh every 15 seconds
  • Interpreter tile always pinned; no recording unless both sides agree
  • Signing must stay readable at 300–500 kbit/s estimate
