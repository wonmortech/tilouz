> **Holat: sinov bosqichi.** Birinchi sinov ilovasi [tilo.wonmor.io/app](https://tilo.wonmor.io/app/) da ([site/app/](site/app/)): yuzma-yuz subtitr, vaziyat kartalari, video xabar va favqulodda karta; telefonda ishlaydi, serverga hech narsa yubormaydi, karta matnlari kar maslahat kengashi tekshiruvini kutmoqda. [Jonli demo](https://wonmor.io/demo/tilo/) esa to'liq rejalashtirilgan ilovaning **konsept demosi**: haqiqiy UI, lekin to'qima ma'lumotlar, imo-ishora videolari o'rnida namuna animatsiya, qayta yuklanganda tiklanadi. Keyingi ishlar [issue'larda](https://github.com/wonmortech/tilouz/issues).

## Nima qiladi

- **Jonli tarjimon (VRI).** Bir tugma: rozilik, navbat, inson surdotarjimon bilan uch tomonlama videoqo'ng'iroq, ish soatlari ochiq.
- **Yuzma-yuz suhbat.** O'zbek va rus nutqi jonli subtitr bo'ladi, javob yoziladi yoki tayyor ibora bilan ovozga aylanadi.
- **Vaziyat kartalari.** Shifoxona, bank, taksi, politsiya, mahalla uchun 69 karta, kar imo-ishorachi yozgan video bilan, internetsiz ishlaydi.
- **Idora yordamchisi.** Hujjatlar ro'yxati va ariza shablonini tushunarli tilda tayyorlaydi.
- **Video xabar va AAC.** Imo-ishora videosini yozib yuborish; gapira olmaydiganlar uchun belgi-taxta.
- **Ota-onalar va lug'at.** Kar bolaning ota-onasi uchun 20 qisqa dars, lotin va kirillda qidiriladigan imo-ishora lug'ati.
- **Keyingi bosqich (v1).** Tashkilotlar katalogi, SOS mashq rejimi, tarjimon kabineti, donor uchun anonim statistika, qulaylik sozlamalari.

## Tez boshlash

Demoni lokal ochish (Node 24):

```sh
npm ci --ignore-scripts && npm run demo   # http://127.0.0.1:4174/demo/tilo/
```

Sayt (`site/`) build talab qilmaydi: istalgan statik server bilan oching, masalan `npx serve site`.

## English

**Tilo is an open-source, non-profit platform that removes the communication barrier between Deaf, hard-of-hearing and non-speaking people and hearing people.** Uzbekistan first, then Central Asia and the CIS.

**Status: early test.** A first test app runs at [tilo.wonmor.io/app](https://tilo.wonmor.io/app/) ([site/app/](site/app/)): face-to-face captions, situation cards, video messages and an emergency card; it runs on the phone and sends nothing to a Tilo server, card texts await review by the Deaf advisory council. The [live demo](https://wonmor.io/demo/tilo/) is a **concept demo** of the full planned app: real UI, fictional data, sample animations in place of signed videos; it resets on reload.

The MVP: one-tap video calls to human sign-language interpreters, live Uzbek/Russian captions for face-to-face conversations, situation cards signed by Deaf signers, an office/petition helper, video messages, AAC for non-speaking people and a learning module for hearing parents. Later: an organisations directory, an SOS channel (drill only in the demo), an interpreter cabinet, anonymous funder statistics, the first open consented Uzbek Sign Language dataset and more CIS languages.

Principles: Deaf-led, free, open (Apache-2.0 code, CC BY 4.0 docs), privacy first, honest about what is planned. Want to help? Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), pick a [`good first issue`](https://github.com/wonmortech/tilouz/labels/good%20first%20issue), and join the [Telegram channel](https://t.me/tiloapp).
