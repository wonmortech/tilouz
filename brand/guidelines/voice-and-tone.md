# Voice and tone

**Tilo talks like a good interpreter: clear, warm, calm, and never in the way.** The conversation belongs to the people; Tilo just makes it possible.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Clear: short sentences, everyday words | Clever: puns, idioms and wordplay that do not translate into sign language |
| Warm and equal: "you" and "we" | Pitying: "suffering from deafness", "despite their disability" |
| Calm, especially in emergencies | Alarming, shouting, ALL CAPS |
| Honest about what works and what does not | Promising "100% accurate" captions |
| Respectful of Deaf culture | Medical by default ("hearing-impaired patients") |

## Words

- Say **Deaf** / **kar** (and the plain Russian word for deaf in Russian copy), **hard of hearing** / **eshitishi past**, **sign language** / **imo-ishora tili**. Ask people how they want to be described.
- Avoid: "deaf-mute", "dumb", "suffers from", "victim", "normal people" (say "hearing people" / "eshituvchilar").
- Sign language is a language, not "gestures".
- In Uzbek use Latin script with the official apostrophes: oʻ gʻ (U+02BB) and the separator ʼ (U+02BC).

## Tone by moment

| Moment | Tone | Example (en) | Example (uz) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome | Warm, short | "Talk to anyone. We'll interpret." | "Har kim bilan gaplashing. Biz tarjima qilamiz." |
| Connecting a call | Calm, specific | "Connecting you to an interpreter. Usually under a minute." | "Tarjimonga ulanmoqda. Odatda bir daqiqadan kam." |
| Error | Honest, gives a way out | "The call dropped. Your interpreter is waiting: reconnect." | "Qo'ng'iroq uzildi. Tarjimon kutmoqda: qayta ulaning." |
| Emergency (SOS) | Calm, direct, no exclamation marks | "Calling emergency services with an interpreter. Stay on this screen." | "Tarjimon bilan favqulodda xizmatga qo'ng'iroq qilinmoqda. Shu ekranda qoling." |
| Captions confidence | Honest | "Captions may contain mistakes. Tap a word to correct it." | "Subtitrlarda xato bo'lishi mumkin. Tuzatish uchun so'zga bosing." |
| Fundraising | Plain, factual | "Every interpreter-minute you fund is a conversation that happened." | "Siz qo'llagan har bir daqiqa - bo'lib o'tgan suhbat." |

Russian versions of these examples are in [`voice-examples.json`](voice-examples.json) and on the `/ru/brand` page.

## Writing for Deaf readers

Many Deaf people learn Uzbek or Russian as a second language after sign language. Write so that it is easy to read and easy to sign: one idea per sentence, subject first, active voice, no double negatives, numbers as digits, and an explanation the first time any technical word appears.
