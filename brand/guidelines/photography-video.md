# Photography and video

## People

- **Real Deaf, hard-of-hearing and non-speaking people**, real interpreters, real situations: a clinic, a bank counter, a classroom, a family kitchen, a bus stop. No stock "person with a hand behind the ear".
- Hands and face are always in frame and in focus when someone signs: sign language uses the face (grammar) as much as the hands. Never crop at the wrists or the chin.
- Eye level, natural light, warm and honest. The Deaf person is the subject of the sentence: shown leading the conversation, not being helped.
- Diversity is the default: ages, regions of Uzbekistan, city and village, women and men, with and without hearing aids or implants.

## Consent

- Written, informed consent in the person's language before any photo or video; for sign-language users the consent is explained in sign language by a qualified interpreter.
- Consent says where the image will appear (site, social, press, dataset) and for how long. **Dataset consent is separate** from marketing consent and can be withdrawn.
- Children only with a parent's consent and the child's own agreement; no identifying details (school, address).
- No photos of real calls or real users' screens. Product screenshots use staged, fictional content.

## Video

- **Captions are always on**, burned in for social video, as a track for the website; Uzbek and Russian, and sign-language interpretation in a picture-in-picture when the video has speech.
- Caption style: Inter 500, white on a `#101214` bar (opacity 0.85 or more), at least 4.5 percent of the frame height, max 2 lines, max 42 characters per line.
- The interpreter window: at least 1/4 of the frame height, never covered by captions or logos, on a plain background.
- Sound is never the only channel: anything said is also signed or written; anything heard (an alarm, a doorbell) is shown.
- The logo ident is the motion intro (1.1 s) at the end, never at the start of a story.

## Colour treatment

Neutral, slightly warm grade. Brand colour comes from the overlay (captions bar, logo), never from tinting the people.
